# GENERAL TIPS

## Subject Line A/B Tests
- Questions outperform statements (47% higher open rate)
- Company name increases open rate by 22%
- Numbers work: "73% faster decisions"
- Avoid spam triggers: "free," "urgent," "limited time"

## Best Send Times
- Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday: 8-10 AM local time
- Avoid Monday (inbox overload) and Friday (checked out)
- Test Sunday evening (6 PM) for executives—inbox is empty

## Follow-Up Cadence
- Email 1: Day 1
- LinkedIn: Day 3
- Email 2: Day 4-5
- Email 3: Day 9-10
- Move to nurture: Day 15

## Personalization Triggers
- Recent funding round
- New executive hire
- Earnings call mention of "AI" or "data"
- Competitor news
- Regulatory news in their industry
- Recent acquisition or expansion

## Reply Handling
- Any response (even "not interested") = success
- "Not now" → Ask when and calendar a follow-up
- "Send more info" → Send ONE thing, ask for meeting
- "Talk to [someone else]" → Ask for intro, cc them
- No response after 3 touches → Move to monthly nurture
