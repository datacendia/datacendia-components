| Enterprise | ¥1,500,000 |
| Sovereign | ¥2,500,000+ |

- **Sovereign deployment MANDATORY** (data localization laws)

**Emphasize:** Local deployment, no foreign data transfer

Focus on other APAC markets first (Singapore, Japan, Australia). Enter China with local partner and dedicated resources when ready.
