## 🎯 RECORDING TIPS

### Before Recording
1. **Clean your demo environment** - Use demo credentials
2. **Pre-load data** - Have deliberations/metrics ready
3. **Close notifications** - No email popups!
4. **Test audio** - Use a good microphone

### During Recording
1. **Move slowly** - Viewers need time to process
2. **Use cursor highlights** - Circle or highlight clicks
3. **Pause on key screens** - Let data sink in
4. **Speak clearly** - Enthusiasm, not hype

### After Recording
1. **Add background music** - Subtle, professional
2. **Include captions** - Accessibility + sound-off viewing
3. **Add end card** - Logo + CTA + URL
