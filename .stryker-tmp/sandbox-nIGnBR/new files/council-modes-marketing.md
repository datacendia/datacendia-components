## See The Difference

**Same question. Three modes. Three completely different outputs.**

### Question: "Should we hire 5 more engineers?"

**⚔️ War Room Mode:**
> CFO attacks the budget impact. COO defends capacity needs. Risk quantifies downside scenarios. Council reaches conditional approval with specific triggers.

**💰 Investment Mode:**
> Full ROI calculation. Scenario analysis (best/base/worst). Comparison to alternatives (hire vs. contract vs. delay). NPV and payback period.

**🚨 Crisis Mode:**
> Immediate action items with owners and deadlines. Communication plan. Escalation triggers. Decision made and executed in the session.
