## 1. 💀 THE PRE-MORTEM

### The Pitch
> "Before you decide, let me show you every way this could fail."

### How It Works
1. User inputs a pending decision ("Expand into European market Q2 2025")
2. The Council runs a pre-mortem simulation
3. AI agents roleplay the future where the decision **FAILED**
4. They work backward to explain exactly why it failed

### Sample Output
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PRE-MORTEM ANALYSIS: European Expansion Q2 2025                │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  FAILURE MODE                    PROBABILITY    COST IMPACT     │
│  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  │
│  1. Regulatory delays            73%           $2.1M            │
│  2. Talent acquisition gap       61%           $890K            │
│  3. Currency exposure            54%           $1.4M            │
│  4. Partner misalignment         48%           $620K            │
│  5. Cultural integration         42%           $340K            │
│  6. Tech stack incompatibility   31%           $780K            │
│  7. Competitive response         28%           $1.8M            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  TOTAL RISK-WEIGHTED EXPOSURE: $4.2M                            │
│  RECOMMENDATION: Proceed with mitigations for items 1-3         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Why It Closes Deals
| Reason | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Based on Gary Klein's actual decision science research | Credibility with sophisticated buyers |
| Executives have been burned before | Speaks directly to their fear |
| No competitor does this | Clear differentiation |
| Quantifies risk in dollars | CFO-friendly |

### Demo Script

**Prospect:** "We're deciding whether to acquire a competitor."

**Sales Rep:** *runs Pre-Mortem* "Here are the 7 ways this acquisition fails, ranked by probability. Do you want to see the Council's full analysis on item #1?"

## 3. 📊 DECISION DEBT DASHBOARD

### The Pitch
> "See every decision that's stuck, who's blocking it, and what it's costing you per day."

### How It Works
A real-time visual dashboard showing:

- **All pending decisions** across the organization
- **Days stuck** for each decision
- **Cost of delay** (calculated in dollars)
- **Who's blocking** (with patterns identified)
- **Dependencies** between decisions
- **Decision Debt Score** for the whole company
