# DEMO NARRATIVE — FLAGSHIP CREDIT DECISION

**Audience:** CRO, Model Risk, Internal Audit, Regulators, Tier-1 Investors  
**Duration:** 15–20 minutes  
**Objective:** Demonstrate decision verification infrastructure for regulated credit decisions

---

## Step 1: Credit Decision (Baseline Reality)

We begin with a standard credit decision evaluated under a formal Credit Committee governance mode.

- A single credit proposal is submitted
- The system evaluates risk, compliance, and policy constraints
- A structured decision record is produced

**Point to emphasize:**

> "This mirrors how regulated institutions already operate — but without altering production systems."

---

## Step 2: What Existing Systems Cannot Provide

At this point, most institutions can answer *what* decision was made.

They cannot reliably answer:

- **Why** it was made
- **Which constraints** were enforced
- **What dissent** existed
- **Whether the same decision can be reproduced later**

**Point to emphasize:**

> "This is not failure — it is simply not what current systems were built to do."

---

## Step 3: What Datacendia Produces Instead

Datacendia generates a regulator-grade decision packet that includes:

| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Decision Summary** | Who decided, what was decided, when |
| **Compliance Mapping** | Basel III, SR 11-7 control attestation |
| **Full Deliberation Record** | Complete reasoning chain |
| **Approval Chain** | Cryptographically signed approvals |
| **Explicit Dissent** | Objections, grounds, and resolution |
| **Integrity Proof** | SHA-256 hash proving nothing has changed |

**Point to emphasize:**

> "This packet is designed to be handed directly to regulators, internal audit, or legal counsel. No interpretation layer required."

---

## Step 4: Deterministic Replay

The same decision is replayed:

- Using the **original inputs**
- Under the **original constraints**
- Producing an **identical outcome**

**Point to emphasize:**

> "This proves the decision was systematic, governed, and reproducible — not ad hoc or post-rationalized."

---

## Step 5: Dissent as a First-Class Artifact

Finally, we show dissent records:

- **Who** objected
- **On what grounds**
- **At what point** in the process
- **Why the decision proceeded anyway**

**Point to emphasize:**

> "This is not noise — it is legal protection. Dissent demonstrates human oversight and institutional deliberation, which is exactly what regulators and courts expect."

---

## Closing Statement

> "Datacendia does not make decisions. Your people, your models, your policies, your authority. We verify that when someone asks 'Why did you decide this?', you have an answer that holds up — in an exam, in a courtroom, or in a board meeting three years from now."
