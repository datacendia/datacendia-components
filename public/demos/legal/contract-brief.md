# CASE BRIEF: Enterprise SaaS Agreement Review

**Client:** Acme Manufacturing Corp.  
**Vendor:** CloudScale Enterprise Solutions, Inc.  
**Agreement Type:** Master Services Agreement + SaaS Order Form  
**Contract Value:** $2.4M over 3 years  
**Review Date:** January 2024

---

## TRANSACTION OVERVIEW

Acme Manufacturing is evaluating a 3-year enterprise software agreement with CloudScale for their ERP and supply chain management platform. The deal includes:

- **Year 1:** $650,000 (implementation + license)
- **Year 2:** $875,000 (full deployment + additional modules)
- **Year 3:** $875,000 (renewal + support)
- **Total:** $2,400,000

---

## KEY CONTRACT TERMS FOR REVIEW

### 1. Pricing & Payment

**Current Terms:**
- 100% of Year 1 fees due upon signing
- Annual payments due 30 days before each contract year
- 8% annual price escalator for renewals
- No refunds for early termination

**Risk Assessment:** HIGH
- Upfront payment creates leverage imbalance
- 8% escalator exceeds market standard (3-5%)
- No termination refund despite SaaS model

### 2. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Current Terms:**
- 99.5% uptime guarantee (monthly calculation)
- Credits: 5% of monthly fee per 1% below SLA
- Credit cap: 25% of monthly fees
- Exclusions: Scheduled maintenance, third-party issues, "force majeure"

**Risk Assessment:** MEDIUM
- 99.5% is below enterprise standard (99.9%)
- Credit cap limits remedies
- Broad exclusions reduce effective guarantee
- No termination right for chronic SLA failures

### 3. Data Security & Privacy

**Current Terms:**
- CloudScale maintains "commercially reasonable" security
- SOC 2 Type II certification (current)
- Data encrypted "in transit" (no mention of at-rest)
- 72-hour breach notification
- Data location: "CloudScale's data centers" (unspecified)

**Risk Assessment:** HIGH
- "Commercially reasonable" is vague
- No encryption at rest specified
- 72 hours exceeds GDPR requirement (some interpretations)
- No geographic data residency commitment
- No right to audit

### 4. Data Ownership & Portability

**Current Terms:**
- Customer "owns" its data
- CloudScale has "license to use data to provide services and improve products"
- Data export: CSV format, 30-day window after termination
- No data deletion commitment post-termination

**Risk Assessment:** HIGH
- Broad license for "product improvement" may include AI training
- CSV-only export may lose data relationships
- 30-day window is short for enterprise migration
- No guaranteed deletion creates compliance risk

### 5. Intellectual Property

**Current Terms:**
- All IP in platform remains CloudScale's
- Customer configurations are "derivative works" owned by CloudScale
- Customer grants CloudScale right to use "anonymized insights" derived from usage

**Risk Assessment:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- Configurations as "derivative works" is aggressive
- "Anonymized insights" may include competitive intelligence
- No carve-out for customer's pre-existing IP

### 6. Limitation of Liability

**Current Terms:**
- Direct damages capped at 12 months of fees paid
- NO liability for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages
- Exceptions: None specified

**Risk Assessment:** HIGH
- 12-month cap may be inadequate for enterprise disruption
- No carve-out for data breach, gross negligence, or IP infringement
- Asymmetric (customer's liability to CloudScale is uncapped)

### 7. Indemnification

**Current Terms:**
- CloudScale indemnifies for third-party IP claims
- Customer indemnifies for: data content, misuse, breach of agreement
- Customer's indemnity is uncapped
- CloudScale's indemnity capped at contract value

**Risk Assessment:** MEDIUM
- Asymmetric caps favor vendor
- Customer data indemnity is broad
- No mutual indemnification for breaches

### 8. Termination

**Current Terms:**
- 3-year initial term, auto-renews for 1-year periods
- 90-day notice required to prevent auto-renewal
- Termination for cause: 60-day cure period
- No termination for convenience
- No refund of prepaid fees upon termination

**Risk Assessment:** HIGH
- No termination for convenience is unusual for SaaS
- 90-day notice window is aggressive
- No refund provision creates lock-in
- 60-day cure period is long

### 9. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

**Current Terms:**
- Delaware law governs
- Mandatory arbitration (AAA Commercial Rules)
- Arbitration in San Francisco, CA
- Prevailing party entitled to attorney's fees
- Waiver of jury trial
- Waiver of class action

**Risk Assessment:** MEDIUM
- Arbitration may limit discovery
- San Francisco venue favors vendor
- Class action waiver is standard but limits remedies

## PRIOR RELEVANT CASES

### SaaS Contract Disputes
- **Rimini Street v. Oracle**, 139 S. Ct. 873 (2019)
  - Software licensing and support obligations

- **Jacobsen v. Katzer**, 535 F.3d 1373 (Fed. Cir. 2008)
  - Open source licensing and derivative works

### Limitation of Liability
- **Schurman Retail Group v. Digi Int'l**, 152 F. Supp. 3d 775 (D. Minn. 2015)
  - Enforceability of liability caps in software agreements

### Data Breach Liability
- **In re Capital One Consumer Data Security Breach Litigation**, 488 F. Supp. 3d 374 (E.D. Va. 2020)
  - Vendor liability for data breaches
