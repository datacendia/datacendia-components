## 6. CHANGE OF CONTROL

**⚠️ SECTION 15.3 - ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL**

**(a) Assignment**
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that either party may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets.

**(b) Change of Control Notification**
Subscriber shall notify Salesforce in writing within **thirty (30) days** of any Change of Control event.

**(c) Termination Right**
**Upon a Change of Control of Subscriber, Salesforce may, at its sole discretion:**
- Continue this Agreement with the successor entity under existing terms
- Require renegotiation of pricing and terms
- **Terminate this Agreement within thirty (30) days of receiving notice of the Change of Control, with no penalty to Salesforce**

**(d) Data Portability**
In the event of termination due to Change of Control, Subscriber shall have sixty (60) days to export all Subscriber Data.
