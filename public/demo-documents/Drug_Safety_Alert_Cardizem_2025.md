## 1. SUMMARY OF CHANGES

The FDA has approved updated dosing guidelines for Diltiazem Hydrochloride (Cardizem) based on post-marketing surveillance data and recent clinical studies.

### Key Changes:

| Parameter | Previous Guideline | Updated Guideline |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Maximum IV bolus | 25 mg | **20 mg** |
| Infusion rate (initial) | 10 mg/hour | **5 mg/hour** |
| Infusion rate (max) | 15 mg/hour | **10 mg/hour** |
| Geriatric adjustment | None specified | **Reduce by 50%** |
| Renal impairment (CrCl <30) | Use with caution | **Contraindicated** |

---

## 2. CLINICAL RATIONALE

### 2.1 Post-Marketing Adverse Events

Analysis of 847 adverse event reports (January 2023 - September 2025) revealed:

| Event Type | Cases | Severity |
|------------|-------|----------|
| Severe bradycardia | 312 | Life-threatening |
| Hypotension requiring intervention | 245 | Serious |
| Complete heart block | 156 | Life-threatening |
| Cardiac arrest | 89 | Fatal (23 cases) |
| Hepatotoxicity | 45 | Serious |

### 2.2 Risk Factors Identified

Patients at elevated risk include:
1. Age ≥75 years (3.2x increased risk)
2. Concurrent beta-blocker therapy (4.1x increased risk)
3. Renal impairment CrCl <30 mL/min (2.8x increased risk)
4. Hepatic impairment Child-Pugh B or C (2.4x increased risk)
5. Concomitant CYP3A4 inhibitors (2.1x increased risk)

## 3. UPDATED PRESCRIBING INFORMATION

### 3.1 Intravenous Administration

**Acute Atrial Fibrillation/Flutter:**
- Initial bolus: 0.25 mg/kg (max 20 mg) over 2 minutes
- If inadequate response after 15 minutes: 0.35 mg/kg (max 25 mg)
- Continuous infusion: Start at 5 mg/hour, titrate to effect
- Maximum infusion rate: 10 mg/hour
- Maximum duration: 24 hours

**Geriatric Patients (≥75 years):**
- Initial bolus: 0.125 mg/kg (max 10 mg)
- Infusion: Start at 2.5 mg/hour, max 5 mg/hour
- Mandatory cardiac monitoring for 6 hours post-infusion

### 3.2 Oral Administration

**Immediate Release:**
- Initial: 30 mg four times daily
- Titrate: Increase at 1-2 day intervals
- Maximum: 360 mg/day in divided doses

**Extended Release:**
- Initial: 120-180 mg once daily
- Titrate: Increase at 7-14 day intervals
- Maximum: 360 mg once daily

## 4. CONTRAINDICATIONS (UPDATED)

### Absolute Contraindications:
1. Sick sinus syndrome (without pacemaker)
2. Second or third-degree AV block (without pacemaker)
3. Severe hypotension (SBP <90 mmHg)
4. Acute myocardial infarction with pulmonary congestion
5. **NEW: Severe renal impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min)**
6. **NEW: Concomitant IV beta-blocker within 2 hours**

### Relative Contraindications:
1. Moderate hepatic impairment
2. Concurrent strong CYP3A4 inhibitors
3. Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (<40%)

---

## 5. DRUG INTERACTIONS (UPDATED)

### 5.1 Contraindicated Combinations

| Drug | Interaction | Action |
|------|-------------|--------|
| IV Beta-blockers | Severe bradycardia, AV block | **Do not administer within 2 hours** |
| Dantrolene | Hyperkalemia, cardiovascular collapse | **Absolute contraindication** |
| Ivabradine | Severe bradycardia | **Contraindicated** |

### 5.2 Major Interactions Requiring Dose Adjustment

| Drug | Effect | Diltiazem Adjustment |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| Simvastatin | Increased statin levels | Max simvastatin 10 mg/day |
| Cyclosporine | Increased cyclosporine levels | Monitor levels, reduce dose 30-50% |
| Carbamazepine | Increased carbamazepine toxicity | Monitor levels closely |
| Buspirone | Increased buspirone levels | Reduce buspirone dose by 50% |
| Fentanyl | Prolonged respiratory depression | Reduce fentanyl dose, monitor |

## 7. PATIENT COUNSELING POINTS

Patients should be advised to:

1. **Report immediately:**
   - Dizziness or lightheadedness
   - Fainting or near-fainting
   - Unusually slow heartbeat
   - Swelling of ankles or feet
   - Shortness of breath
   - Yellowing of skin or eyes

2. **Avoid:**
   - Grapefruit and grapefruit juice
   - Sudden discontinuation
   - Alcohol in excess

3. **Inform healthcare providers:**
   - All current medications including OTC
   - Any planned dental or surgical procedures
   - Pregnancy or plans to become pregnant
