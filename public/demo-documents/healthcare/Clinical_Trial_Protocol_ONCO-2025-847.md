## 2. STUDY OBJECTIVES

### 2.1 Primary Objective
To compare overall survival (OS) between pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy versus placebo plus chemotherapy in participants with previously untreated metastatic NSCLC.

### 2.2 Secondary Objectives
1. Compare progression-free survival (PFS) per RECIST 1.1
2. Evaluate objective response rate (ORR)
3. Assess duration of response (DOR)
4. Characterize safety and tolerability profile
5. Evaluate patient-reported outcomes (PROs)

### 2.3 Exploratory Objectives
1. Identify predictive biomarkers of response
2. Evaluate ctDNA dynamics as early response marker
3. Assess tumor microenvironment characteristics

### 3.2 Treatment Arms

**Arm A (Experimental):**
- Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV Q3W
- Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² IV Q3W (non-squamous)
- Carboplatin AUC 5 IV Q3W (4 cycles)

**Arm B (Control):**
- Placebo IV Q3W
- Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² IV Q3W (non-squamous)
- Carboplatin AUC 5 IV Q3W (4 cycles)

## 4. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

### 4.1 Inclusion Criteria

1. Age ≥18 years
2. Histologically confirmed metastatic NSCLC (Stage IV)
3. No prior systemic therapy for metastatic disease
4. PD-L1 TPS ≥1% by IHC 22C3 pharmDx assay
5. Measurable disease per RECIST 1.1
6. ECOG Performance Status 0-1
7. Adequate organ function:
   - ANC ≥1,500/μL
   - Platelets ≥100,000/μL
   - Hemoglobin ≥9.0 g/dL
   - Creatinine clearance ≥45 mL/min
   - Total bilirubin ≤1.5× ULN
   - AST/ALT ≤2.5× ULN (≤5× if liver metastases)
8. Life expectancy ≥3 months
9. Written informed consent

### 4.2 Exclusion Criteria

1. EGFR mutation or ALK rearrangement positive
2. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment
3. History of interstitial lung disease or pneumonitis
4. Active infection requiring systemic therapy
5. Known HIV, HBV, or HCV infection (unless controlled)
6. Prior anti-PD-1/PD-L1/CTLA-4 therapy
7. Symptomatic brain metastases (treated stable allowed)
8. Pregnancy or breastfeeding
9. Live vaccine within 30 days of first dose

## 6. SAFETY

### 6.1 Adverse Events of Special Interest (AESI)

| Category | Examples | Management |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Immune-mediated pneumonitis | ILD, pneumonitis | Hold/discontinue, steroids |
| Immune-mediated colitis | Diarrhea, colitis | Hold, steroids, infliximab PRN |
| Immune-mediated hepatitis | Elevated LFTs | Hold/discontinue, steroids |
| Immune-mediated endocrinopathies | Thyroid, adrenal, pituitary | Hormone replacement |
| Immune-mediated nephritis | Elevated creatinine | Hold, steroids |
| Infusion reactions | Hypersensitivity | Premedication, rate reduction |

### 6.2 Dose Modifications

**Pembrolizumab:**
- Grade 2 irAE: Hold until ≤Grade 1
- Grade 3 irAE: Hold, steroids, resume if resolves
- Grade 4 irAE: Permanently discontinue
- Recurrent Grade 3: Permanently discontinue

**Chemotherapy:**
- Per standard institutional guidelines
- Dose reductions permitted per protocol

### 6.3 Stopping Rules

The DSMB may recommend stopping the trial if:
1. Unacceptable toxicity rate (>30% Grade 4+ irAEs)
2. Futility at interim analysis
3. Overwhelming efficacy (O'Brien-Fleming boundary crossed)

## 7. STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS

### 7.1 Sample Size

- Primary endpoint: OS
- Hazard ratio assumption: 0.75 (experimental vs. control)
- Alpha: 0.025 (one-sided)
- Power: 90%
- Median OS control: 12 months
- Required events: 510 deaths
- Sample size: 850 patients (425 per arm)

### 7.2 Interim Analyses

| Analysis | Timing | Purpose |
|----------|--------|---------|
| IA1 | 50% events | Futility only |
| IA2 | 75% events | Efficacy and futility |
| Final | 100% events | Primary analysis |

### 7.3 Analysis Populations

- **ITT**: All randomized patients
- **Safety**: All patients receiving ≥1 dose
- **Per-Protocol**: ITT excluding major protocol violations
