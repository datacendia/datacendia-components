## 2. PRICING AND COMMITMENT

### 2.1 Committed Spend
Customer commits to a minimum annual spend of **$180,000** per year for the term of this Agreement.

### 2.2 Discount Structure
| Annual Spend Tier | Discount |
|-------------------|----------|
| $0 - $100,000 | 0% |
| $100,001 - $200,000 | 15% |
| $200,001 - $500,000 | 22% |
| $500,001+ | 28% |

| Service | Monthly Uptime SLA |

## 4. DATA PROTECTION

### 4.1 Data Location
All Customer data shall be stored in AWS US regions unless otherwise specified.

### 4.2 Encryption
- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption
- Data in transit: TLS 1.2 or higher

### 4.3 Compliance
AWS maintains the following certifications relevant to Customer's use:
- SOC 2 Type II
- ISO 27001
- HIPAA (with BAA)
- PCI DSS Level 1

## 5. CHANGE OF CONTROL PROVISION

**⚠️ IMPORTANT - Section 5.3**

In the event of a Change of Control of Customer (including but not limited to merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets):

(a) Customer shall provide written notice to AWS within **sixty (60) days** of the Change of Control event.

(b) AWS reserves the right to:
   - Review and approve the successor entity's creditworthiness
   - Require updated compliance certifications
   - Adjust pricing based on the successor entity's usage profile

(c) Failure to provide timely notice may result in:
   - Termination of discount pricing
   - Requirement to execute a new agreement at then-current rates

## 6. TERMINATION

### 6.1 For Convenience
Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice, subject to payment of any committed spend shortfall.

### 6.2 For Cause
Either party may terminate immediately upon material breach that remains uncured for 30 days after written notice.

### 6.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- Customer has 30 days to export all data
- AWS will delete Customer data within 90 days of termination
- Any prepaid fees for unused services will be refunded pro-rata
