## The Narrative (Locked)

**Datacendia is decision verification infrastructure for regulated institutions.**

**We do not make decisions. We prove decisions were made correctly.**

---

## What This Means

Datacendia sits between institutional decision-making systems and regulatory accountability.

We do not replace models, underwriters, committees, or human judgment.

We produce the evidence that proves those decisions were:
- Made with the right inputs
- Evaluated against the right policies
- Reviewed by the right authorities
- Documented to regulator-grade standards
- Reproducible months or years later

---

## What We Are NOT

- We are not an AI decision-maker
- We are not a model governance tool
- We are not a workflow automation platform
- We are not a compliance checklist system
- We are not a chatbot or copilot

---

## The Single Sentence

When asked "What does Datacendia do?", the answer is:

> "We produce regulator-grade proof that institutional decisions were made correctly."

No variations. No clever reframes. No new metaphors.

---

## Commercial Beachhead (Locked)

**Tier-1 Financial Institutions — Model Risk & Credit Governance**

This is the only go-to-market vertical.

All other verticals (Legal, Healthcare, Government, Defense, etc.) are:
- Pattern-validated
- Technically complete
- Not go-to-market

Why Financial Services:
- SR 11-7 exists (regulatory mandate)
- Basel III/IV compliance is audited
- Budget owner is identifiable (CRO, Model Risk, Internal Audit)
- Legal precedent favors documentation over automation
- Platform is deployment-complete for this use case

## Demo Flow (Locked)

The correct demo sequence is:

1. "Here is a credit decision regulators already inspect."
2. "Here is how it fails today." (post-hoc reconstruction, missing evidence)
3. "Here is what Datacendia produces instead." (regulator-grade packet)
4. "Here is how it replays, identically, six months later."
5. "Here is the dissent that saved you in court."

**No dashboards first.**  
**No agents first.**  
**No architecture diagrams.**

Institutions buy relief, not capability.
