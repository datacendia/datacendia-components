# CASE STUDY CREATION CHECKLIST

## Before the Interview

- [ ] Identify customer willing to be a reference
- [ ] Get legal approval for case study
- [ ] Schedule 30-minute interview with key stakeholder
- [ ] Prepare questions (see below)
- [ ] Pull usage data from their Datacendia instance

## Interview Questions

**Context:**
1. Tell me about [Company] and your role.
2. What was the situation before Datacendia?
3. What was the breaking point that made you look for a solution?

**Selection:**
4. What other solutions did you consider?
5. Why did you choose Datacendia?
6. What were your initial concerns or hesitations?

**Implementation:**
7. How was the implementation experience?
8. How did your team adopt the platform?
9. Any surprises (good or bad)?

**Results:**
10. What's different now?
11. Can you quantify the impact? (Time saved, costs reduced, etc.)
12. What specific features have been most valuable?
13. Can you share a specific example or story?

**Looking Ahead:**
14. What's next for you with Datacendia?
15. Would you recommend Datacendia? Why?

**Quote:**
16. If you had to summarize the impact in one sentence, what would you say?

## After the Interview

- [ ] Draft case study
- [ ] Get customer approval on quotes and data

- [ ] Design/format case study
- [ ] Create 1-page PDF version
- [ ] Create web page version
