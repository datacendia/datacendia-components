## ANTI-ARBITRAGE POLICY

### Rules to Prevent Gaming

1. **Billing Entity Rule**
   - Pricing based on legal entity location (headquarters)
   - Invoice must go to entity in the designated region

2. **Primary Usage Rule**
   - >70% of active users must be in the designated region
   - Usage audits may be conducted

3. **Multinational Policy**
   - Companies with operations in multiple regions: price based on HQ
   - OR negotiate global enterprise agreement at blended rate

4. **Subsidiary Rule**
   - US company's India subsidiary = US pricing
   - Indian company's US subsidiary = Indian pricing (for India operations)

5. **Migration Policy**
   - If company relocates HQ, pricing adjusts at next renewal
   - 12-month notice required for pricing region change

### Contract Language

> "Regional pricing applies to organizations legally headquartered in and primarily operating within the designated region. 'Primarily operating' means more than 70% of licensed users are located in the region. Organizations with significant operations outside the designated region may be subject to standard pricing or a negotiated blended regional rate. Datacendia reserves the right to audit usage location annually."

## SPECIAL PRICING PROGRAMS

### Founding Customer Program
**For first 5 customers in any region:**
- 50% discount on Year 1
- Standard pricing Year 2+
- Requirements: Case study rights, 3 reference calls, quarterly feedback

### Startup Program
**For companies <$10M revenue, <100 employees:**
- 70% discount on Starter tier only
- Must graduate to standard pricing at $10M revenue or 100 employees

### Non-Profit / Education
**For registered non-profits and educational institutions:**
- 50% discount on all tiers
- Requires verification of status

### Government
**For government entities:**
- Pricing as listed (no automatic discount)
