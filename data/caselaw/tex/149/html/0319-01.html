Morgan Farms et al v. Honorable W. O. Murray, Chief Justice et al.

No. A-2822.

Decided October 11, 1950.

(233 S. W., 2d Series, 123.)

Pickinson, Davis & Hale; McCampbell, Wood & Kirkhan, Norman L. Utter and L. Dewitt Hale, all of Corpus Christi, for petitioner.

Jackson & Jackson and H. S. Groesbeeck, of Crystal City, for respondent.

Per Curiam.

The motion for leave to file the petition for mandamus is overruled because the Court approves the holding of the Court of Civil Appeals. Morgan Farms et al v. Brown, 231 S. W. 2d 790. Rule 475, as amended by Rule effective March 1, 1950.

Opinion delivered October 11, 1950.
