PER CURIAM.

In this civil rights action brought under 42 U.S.C. § 1981, Kasib Bilal appeals following the district court's1 adverse grant *1107of summary judgment. Upon careful de novo review, see Withers v. Dick's Sporting Goods, Inc. , 636 F.3d 958, 962 (8th Cir. 2011), we conclude that the district court did not err in granting summary judgment and we find no other basis for reversal.

Accordingly, we affirm. See 8th Cir. R. 47B.
