No. 12-307.

United States v. Windsor, Executor of the Estate of Spyer, et al.

C. A. 2d Cir. Certiorari granted. In addition to the question presented by the petition, the parties are directed to brief and argue the following questions: 'Whether the Executive Branch’s agreement with the court below that DOMA [Defense of Marriage Act] is unconstitutional deprives this Court of jurisdiction to decide this case; and whether the Bipartisan Legal Advisory Group of the United States House of Representatives has Article III standing in this case.”
