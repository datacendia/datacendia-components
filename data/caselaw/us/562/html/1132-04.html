No. 10-5400.

Tapia v. United States.

C. A. 9th Cir. [Certiorari granted, ante, p. 1104,] Stephanos Bibas, Esq., of Philadelphia, Pa., is invited to brief and argue this case as amicus curiae in support of the position that 18 U. S. C. § 3582(a) allows district courts to consider rehabilitative need in setting the length and term of imprisonment.
