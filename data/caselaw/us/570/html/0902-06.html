In the Matter of DISCIPLINE OF Kevin P. CLAFFEY.

No. D-2728.

Supreme Court of the United States

June 17, 2013.

Kevin P. Claffey, of Brooklyn, New York, is suspended from the practice of law in this Court and a rule will issue, returnable within 40 days, requiring him to show cause why he should not be disbarred from the practice of law in this Court.
