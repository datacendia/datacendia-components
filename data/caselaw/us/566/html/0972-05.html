No. D-2657.

In re Disbarment of Lieberman.

Richard Donald Lieberman, of Bethesda, Md., having requested to resign as a member of the Bar of this Court, it is ordered that his name be stricken from the roll of attorneys permitted to the practice of law before this Court. The rule to show cause, issued on April 16, 2012 [ante, p. 934], is discharged.
