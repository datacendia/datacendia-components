ORDER

Per Curiam:

*918Mr. Maurice R. Hogan appeals his convictions by a Cole County jury for first-degree child molestation, § 566.067, and sexual misconduct involving a child by indecent exposure, § 566.083. The trial court sentenced him to concurrent terms of twenty-five years' imprisonment for child molestation and five years for sexual misconduct. We affirm. Rule 30.25(b).
