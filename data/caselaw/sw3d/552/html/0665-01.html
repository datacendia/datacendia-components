PER CURIAM:

*666Roy Wayne Sinden ("Sinden") appeals his conviction for driving while intoxicated under 577.010, alleging in his sole point that the evidence was insufficient to establish that he "operated" a motor vehicle while intoxicated. The trial court's judgment is affirmed. Because a published opinion would have no precedential value, a memorandum of law has been provided to the parties. Missouri Supreme Court Rule 30.25(b)
