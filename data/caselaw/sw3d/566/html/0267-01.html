ORDER

Per Curiam

John Headley appeals his convictions and sentences for first-degree statutory sodomy and incest. He contends the evidence was insufficient to support the convictions. Upon review of the briefs and the *268record, we find no error and affirm the judgment. We have provided the parties with a Memorandum explaining the reasons for our decision, because a published opinion would serve no jurisprudential purpose.

AFFIRMED. Rule 30.25(b).
