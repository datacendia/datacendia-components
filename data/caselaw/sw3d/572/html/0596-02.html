Order

Per Curiam:

*597Mr. Brett Daffron appeals from the Judgment of the Circuit Court of Andrew County, Missouri, Domestic Division, in favor of Ms. Jane Daffron and denying Mr. Daffron's post-dissolution motion to modify the parenting plan between himself and Ms. Daffron. Because a published opinion would have no precedential value, a memorandum of law has been provided to the parties. The judgment is affirmed. Rule 84.16(b).
