ORDER

Per Curiam:

*596Mr. Ryan Telgemeier appeals a Labor and Industrial Relations Commission (Commission) decision affirming an Appeals Tribunal's conclusion that he was disqualified to receive unemployment compensation benefits because he voluntarily left work without good cause attributable to his work or employer. For the reasons stated in the memorandum provided to the parties, we affirm. Rule 84.16(b).
