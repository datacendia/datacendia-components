PER CURIAM:

Mohamed Mostafa appeals his conviction in the Boone County Circuit Court for rape and sentence of twenty-five years' imprisonment. In his sole point on appeal, he claims the trial court erred in admitting DNA test results into evidence. Mostafa asserts that DNA results were fruit of the poisonous tree. Because a published opinion would have no precedential value, a memorandum has been provided to the parties. The judgment is affirmed. Rule 30.25(b).
