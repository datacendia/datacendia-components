ORDER

Per Curiam

Ted Turner appeals the circuit court's judgment dissolving his marriage to Teresa Turner. He brings three points challenging the division of property. Upon review of the briefs and the record, we find no error and affirm the judgment. The parties have been provided with a Memorandum explaining the reasons for our decision, because a published opinion would have no precedential value.

AFFIRMED. Rule 84.16(b)
