ORDER

PER CURIAM:

*865Operational Solutions appeals the judgment of the Jackson County Circuit Court overruling its motion to vacate/quash a foreign judgment. It complains on appeal that the New Jersey court should not have entered the judgment at issue because it did not have personal jurisdiction over Operational Solutions. Because a published opinion would have no precedential value, a memorandum has been provided to the parties. The judgment is affirmed. Rule 84.16(b).
