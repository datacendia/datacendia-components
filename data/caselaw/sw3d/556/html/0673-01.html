ORDER

PER CURIAM.

Yvette Peebles appeals the decision of the Labor and Industrial Relations Commission ("the Commission") denying her unemployment benefits. We find no error has occurred.

No jurisprudential purpose would be served by a written opinion. We have, however, provided the parties a memorandum setting forth the reasons for our decision.

*674The decision of the Commission is affirmed under Rule 84.16(b).
