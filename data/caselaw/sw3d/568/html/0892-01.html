Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District.

Joshua D. Hawley, Attorney General, and Garrick Aplin, Assistant Attorney General, Jefferson City, MO, Attorneys for Respondent.

Amanda Langenheim, Assistant Public Defender, Kansas City, MO, Attorney for Appellant.

Before Division Four: Karen King Mitchell, Chief Judge, and Victor C. Howard and Lisa White Hardwick, Judges

Order

Per Curiam:

Charles Henderson appeals, following a bench trial, his conviction of the class A misdemeanor of violating an order of protection under § 455.085.8, RSMo, Supp. 2014, for which he was sentenced to one year in the county jail with credit for time served. Finding no error, plain or otherwise, we affirm. Rule 30.25(b).
