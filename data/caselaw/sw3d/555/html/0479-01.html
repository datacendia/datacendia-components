ORDER

Per Curiam *480Michael McKinzy ("Father") appeals from the circuit court's judgment ordering him to pay a combined arrearage of child support and maintenance to his former wife, Carletha McKinzy ("Mother"). Father contends the court erred in denying his request to set aside the original administrative order for his child support obligation as void due to lack of proper service of process in the administrative proceeding. For reasons explained herein, we affirm.

AFFIRMED. Rule 84.16(b)
