## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, understand how you use it, and improve your experience. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixel tags.

---

## 2. How We Use Cookies

Datacendia uses cookies on datacendia.com and app.datacendia.com for the following purposes:

### 2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Services to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|--------|---------|----------|------|
| `dc_session` | Maintains your authenticated session | Session | First-party |
| `dc_csrf` | Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks | Session | First-party |
| `dc_cookie_consent` | Stores your cookie consent preferences | 1 year | First-party |

### 2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences and settings.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|--------|---------|----------|------|
| `dc_theme` | Stores your light/dark mode preference | 1 year | First-party |
| `dc_locale` | Stores your language preference | 1 year | First-party |
| `dc_sidebar` | Remembers sidebar collapsed/expanded state | 1 year | First-party |

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. All analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|--------|---------|----------|------|
| `dc_analytics` | Anonymous usage analytics (self-hosted, no third-party) | 1 year | First-party |

**Note:** We do **not** use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking cookies. All analytics are processed on our own infrastructure.

### 2.4 Cookies We Do NOT Use

- No advertising or retargeting cookies
- No cross-site tracking cookies
- No social media tracking pixels
- No third-party analytics that share data externally

## 3. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You can:

- **Accept All** — Enable all cookie categories
- **Reject Non-Essential** — Only strictly necessary cookies are used
- **Customize** — Choose which categories to enable

You can change your preferences at any time via **Settings > Privacy** in the application or by clearing your browser cookies.

---

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not set third-party cookies. If you enable enterprise connectors (e.g., Slack, Microsoft Teams), those third-party services may set their own cookies when you authenticate with them. Please refer to their respective cookie policies.

---

## 5. Local Storage and Session Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage and session storage for:

| Key | Purpose | Storage Type |
|-----|---------|-------------|
| `dc_auth_token` | Authentication token for API requests | Session storage |
| `dc_draft_*` | Auto-save of in-progress deliberations | Local storage |
| `dc_feature_flags` | Cached feature flag state | Local storage |

These are cleared when you log out or clear your browser data.

---

## 6. How to Control Cookies

### Browser Settings
Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies via settings:
- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings > Privacy > Cookies

### Impact of Disabling Cookies
If you disable strictly necessary cookies, the Services may not function correctly. Disabling functional or analytics cookies will not affect core functionality.

## 7. Self-Hosted and Sovereign Deployments

For Self-Hosted and Sovereign (Air-Gapped) deployments:

- Analytics cookies (if enabled) are processed entirely on your servers
