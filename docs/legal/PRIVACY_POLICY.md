## 1. Introduction

Datacendia, Inc. ("Datacendia," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our customers, users, and website visitors. This policy applies to:
- Visitors to datacendia.com and app.datacendia.com
- Users of the Datacendia platform (all tiers)
- Contacts who interact with our sales, support, or marketing teams

For Self-Hosted and Sovereign (Air-Gapped) deployments, Sections 4–6 apply only to data that transits Datacendia-controlled infrastructure (e.g., license validation, support requests). Customer Data processed entirely on Customer infrastructure is not collected or accessed by Datacendia.

## 9. Your Rights

### 9.1 GDPR Rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification** — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure** — Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction** — Request we limit processing of your data
- **Portability** — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Object** — Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdraw Consent** — Where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@datacendia.com. We respond within 30 days.

### 9.2 CCPA/CPRA Rights (California Residents)

You have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information we collect and why
- **Delete** your personal information
- **Opt-out** of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal data)
- **Non-discrimination** for exercising your rights
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information
- **Limit use** of sensitive personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than those permitted by the CPRA.

### 9.3 Other Jurisdictions

We honor data subject rights under applicable local laws, including but not limited to Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, and Australia's Privacy Act. Contact privacy@datacendia.com for requests.
