> **Last Updated:** February 2026

> **Goal:** 5-10 signed design partners within 90 days
> **Design Partner Value Prop:** Free/discounted platform access in exchange for feedback, case study rights, and logo usage

---

## What is a Design Partner?

A design partner is an organization that:
1. **Has the problem** — deploying AI and needs governance infrastructure
2. **Feels the urgency** — regulatory pressure, board demands, or recent governance incident
3. **Has budget authority** — can convert to a paid customer after the design partnership
4. **Will co-develop** — provides feedback, participates in product reviews, allows case study
5. **Provides credibility** — their logo and testimony validate the platform for investors and other customers

---

## How to Use This Document

Each entry includes:
- **Why them** — specific governance need
- **Who to contact** — the executive most likely to champion
- **The angle** — what makes Datacendia uniquely valuable to THEM
- **What to say** — opening message tailored to their situation
- **Design partner offer** — what you give them / what you ask for
- **Status** — tracking field
