# Outreach Playbook (Solo Founder Edition)

Use these templates as copy-paste starting points, then personalize before sending.

## 1) Finding Direct Emails

Use lightweight tooling first:

- **Hunter.io** free tier for domain patterns and confidence scoring
- **Apollo.io** free tier for contact discovery and role filtering

Workflow:
1. Identify exact title + company domain.
2. Pull 1-3 likely email formats.
3. Verify against public patterns (press releases, blog bylines, conference pages).
4. Send one clean message from your primary founder email.

## 2) Personalise Before Sending

Do at least one specific personalization line:

- A recent post they wrote
- A conference talk they gave
- A policy/technical article they shared

Keep it short and real. You're proving this is not bulk spam.

## 3) When They Reply

Reply same day if possible.

1. Thank them briefly.
2. Send the relevant platform demonstration PDF/link.
3. Offer a **15-minute call, no deck**.
4. Suggest two concrete time windows.

## 4) When They Don't Reply

- Send **one** follow-up after 7 days.
- If no response, move on.
- Don't create a long drip sequence while you're pre-PMF.

## 5) Weekly Output Target

A realistic pace for a solo founder:

- **20 personalized outreach messages per week**
- Track: sent, replied, positive replies, calls booked
- Iterate on subject lines based on reply rate, not opens

## 6) Where to Post Content

- Hacker News (Show HN / Ask HN)
- LinkedIn (founder account + targeted comments)
- r/MachineLearning
- r/netsec

Post technical artifacts and decision examples, not generic product claims.
