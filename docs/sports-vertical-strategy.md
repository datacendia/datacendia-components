## Crisis Immunization Framework

### 5 Missing Primitives (What institutions lack)
1. **Discovery-Time Proof** — When was evidence actionable?
2. **Deliberation Capture** — How was the decision made?
3. **Override Accountability** — Who overrode whom, and why?
4. **Continuity Memory** — What leaves when leaders leave?
5. **Drift Detection** — When does compliance slip before audit?

### 8 Crisis Immunization Modules

| # | Module | Problem Class | Primary Target |
