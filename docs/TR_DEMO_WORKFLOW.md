# HARD TR PARTNER QUESTIONS (With Answers)

## Q1: "Isn't this just making decisions with extra steps?"

**Answer:**
> "No. Humans remain accountable. Datacendia structures deliberation, enforces controls, and preserves proof. It's a decision verification platform, not a decision maker."

---

## Q2: "How is this different from logging or observability tools?"

**Answer:**
> "Logs are after-the-fact. Datacendia operates before execution — it evaluates intent, surfaces dissent, and creates evidence before the decision is made. That's the difference between a flight recorder and a pre-flight checklist."

---

## Q3: "Does this compete with CoCounsel or Harvey?"

**Answer:**
> "No. Those are AI tools that assist with legal work. Datacendia is infrastructure that makes any AI-assisted decision defensible. We're vendor-agnostic, model-agnostic. We don't care which AI tool you use — we care that you can prove how it was governed."

---

## Q4: "Does this give legal advice?"

**Answer:**
> "Absolutely not. Datacendia is opinionated about process, not outcomes. We structure how decisions are evaluated and preserve evidence of that process. We never recommend what to decide."

---

## Q5: "Why would TR care about this?"

**Answer:**
> "TR owns authoritative content and regulatory intelligence. What TR doesn't own is how AI decisions are governed inside enterprises. Datacendia fills that gap — and we treat Westlaw as authoritative evidence inside the verification process, not as a competitor."

---

## Q6: "What if the AI is wrong?"

**Answer:**
> "That's exactly why Datacendia exists. When AI is wrong, you need to prove you had controls in place. Datacendia preserves the dissent, the risk flags, the human override — so when something goes wrong, you can show you weren't negligent."

---

## Q7: "How do you handle hallucinations?"

**Answer:**
> "Every AI statement is attributed, timestamped, and hashed. If an agent hallucinates, that hallucination is recorded — along with any dissent or challenge from other agents. The system doesn't prevent hallucinations; it makes them auditable and containable."

---

## Q8: "Is this admissible in court?"

**Answer:**
> "We generate cryptographically signed evidence with Merkle tree integrity proofs. The format is designed for legal discovery. Whether it's admissible depends on jurisdiction and case law, but we've built it to the standard that enterprise legal teams expect."

---

## Q9: "What's your business model?"

**Answer:**
> "Enterprise SaaS. Per-seat or per-decision pricing depending on deployment. We're exploring partnership models where Datacendia is bundled with existing compliance or legal tech stacks."

---

## Q10: "Why should we partner instead of build this ourselves?"

**Answer:**
> "You could build it. But this is infrastructure, not content. It's not your core competency, and it's not where your editorial authority lives. A partnership lets TR extend into AI governance without distracting from what makes Westlaw valuable."

# THE STORY: "The Meridian Capital Transfer"

## Scenario Overview

**Customer:** Meridian Capital Partners (fictional large financial institution)

**Regulatory Obligations:**
- SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission)
- FINRA (Financial Industry Regulatory Authority)  
- Basel III (Banking capital requirements)
- Internal Audit Committee oversight

**The Situation:**
At 3:15 PM EST, Treasury Bot initiates a $2.5M fund transfer request involving:
- **Recipient:** Viktor Petrov — flagged as a Politically Exposed Person (PEP)
- **Destination:** Cyprus-based holding company (cross-border jurisdiction)

- **Complication:** Petrov is a board member of a client's subsidiary

**The Risk:**
If this transfer is challenged in 2 years by SEC, FINRA, or in litigation, Meridian must prove:
1. Due diligence occurred before execution
2. Risks were identified and documented
3. Dissent was surfaced and recorded
4. Leadership knowingly approved (or rejected) with full awareness

# HOW UPDATES WORK (Regulator-Safe Explanation)

## The Rule

> **Decisions are immutable. Updates are additive. Corrections never overwrite.**

## Where Updates Live

Updates do **NOT** touch Steps 1-9 (pre-decision and decision-time).

Updates live in:

### Step 10 — Post-Decision Review & Monitoring

- New intelligence (e.g., Petrov added to sanctions list)
- Regulatory guidance changes
- Market condition changes
- Recorded as **Monitoring Events**, linked to original decision hash

### Step 11 — Corrective Action & Improvement

- Transfer paused or reversed
- Policy updated
- Recorded as **Corrective Action Records** with human authorization
