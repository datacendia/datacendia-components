## Jurisdiction-by-Jurisdiction Mapping

### 1. EUROPEAN UNION — EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Effective Dates** | Prohibited practices: Feb 2025. High-risk obligations: **Aug 2, 2026**. General-purpose AI: Aug 2025 |
| **Who's Affected** | Any organization deploying AI in the EU or affecting EU persons — regardless of where headquartered |
| **Penalties** | Up to **€35M or 7% of global annual turnover** (Art. 99(3)) for prohibited practices; €15M/3% for high-risk violations |
| **Key Obligations** | Risk classification, conformity assessment, technical documentation, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, accuracy/robustness monitoring |
| **Datacendia Coverage** | EU AI Act Engine (Art. 5-50 classification), ImmutableAuditLedger (Art. 12), Council deliberation capture (Art. 13), CendiaVetoService (Art. 14), BiasDetection (Art. 10), PostQuantumKMS (Art. 15), FRIA automation (Art. 27), conformity assessment routing (Art. 43) |
| **Urgency** | 🔴 **17 months to high-risk deadline** |

### 2. EUROPEAN UNION — DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Effective Date** | **January 17, 2025** (already in force) |
| **Who's Affected** | Financial entities: banks, insurance, investment firms, payment institutions, crypto-asset providers operating in EU |
| **Penalties** | Up to **1% of average daily worldwide turnover** for each day of non-compliance; critical third-party providers face up to €5M (or 10% of turnover) |
| **Key Obligations** | ICT risk management framework, incident reporting (24hr initial / 72hr intermediate / 1 month final), digital operational resilience testing, third-party risk management |
| **Datacendia Coverage** | ContinuousComplianceMonitor (ICT risk monitoring), ImmutableAuditLedger (incident evidence), CendiaSentry (threat detection), Governance Receipt (audit trail for third-party AI) |
| **Urgency** | 🔴 **Already enforceable** |

### 3. PERU — DS N° 115-2025-PCM + Ley 31814

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Effective Dates** | DS 115-2025-PCM: 2025 (adopts ISO/IEC 42001:2023 as mandatory standard). Ley 31814: 2023 (AI regulatory framework) |
| **Who's Affected** | All organizations deploying AI in Peru. Credit scoring, judicial decisions, and public services classified as **high-risk** under Ley 31814 Art. 8 |
| **Penalties** | SBS sanctions for financial institutions: suspension of operations, fines, board-level accountability. ANPD sanctions for data protection violations under Ley 29733 |
| **Key Obligations** | ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system, risk assessment for high-risk AI, transparency and explainability, human oversight, non-discrimination, data protection (Ley 29733) |
| **Datacendia Coverage** | CendiaGateway (reverse-proxy audit of all AI interactions), DS 115-2025-PCM evidence package generation, DPIA template (Ley 29733), AI Manifest per interaction, PII detection, sovereign deployment (data stays on-premises) |
| **Urgency** | 🔴 **Already enforceable — SBS actively supervising** |

### 4. UNITED STATES — NIST AI RMF + State Laws + SEC

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Effective Dates** | NIST AI RMF: Voluntary but becoming de facto standard. Colorado AI Act: **Feb 1, 2026**. SEC Reg BI/Cyber rules: Ongoing. Executive Order 14110 (Oct 2023) |
| **Who's Affected** | Colorado AI Act: deployers of high-risk AI in consumer decisions. SEC: registered entities. Federal agencies: EO 14110 requirements |
| **Penalties** | Colorado: AG enforcement, $20K per violation. SEC: varies by rule. Federal procurement: contract exclusion for non-compliant AI |
| **Key Obligations** | Risk management (NIST AI RMF 4 functions), impact assessments, algorithmic discrimination prevention, disclosure requirements, model documentation |
| **Datacendia Coverage** | Full NIST AI RMF implementation (GOVERN/MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE), CognitiveBiasMitigation, 30 vertical-specific risk profiles, CendiaCrucible (adversarial testing) |
| **Urgency** | 🟡 **Colorado deadline passed; federal framework crystallizing** |

### 5. UNITED KINGDOM — FCA/PRA AI Framework

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Effective Dates** | FCA Discussion Paper DP5/22 (AI in financial services). AI Safety Institute: operational 2024. UK AI Regulation white paper: principles-based, sector-led |
| **Who's Affected** | FCA/PRA-regulated financial services firms deploying AI |
| **Penalties** | FCA: unlimited fines. PRA: unlimited fines + senior manager accountability under SM&CR |
| **Key Obligations** | Safety, transparency, fairness, accountability, contestability. Model risk management (SS1/23). Consumer Duty outcomes for AI-driven decisions |
| **Datacendia Coverage** | Council deliberation (transparency), CendiaVeto (contestability), ImmutableAuditLedger (accountability), SR 11-7 equivalent model documentation |
| **Urgency** | 🟡 **FCA actively reviewing AI use — enforcement actions likely 2026-2027** |

### 6. BRAZIL — LGPD + AI Bill (PL 2338/2023)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Effective Dates** | LGPD: In force since 2020. AI Bill (PL 2338/2023): Expected passage 2026 |
| **Who's Affected** | All organizations processing data of Brazilian persons (LGPD). AI Bill: deployers of high-risk AI systems |
| **Penalties** | LGPD: up to **2% of revenue in Brazil, capped at R$50M per infraction**. AI Bill: TBD but expected to follow EU AI Act penalty structure |
| **Key Obligations** | Algorithmic impact assessments, transparency in automated decisions, right to human review, data protection by design |
| **Datacendia Coverage** | GDPR/LGPD DSR endpoints (export, erasure, rectification, portability), impact assessment automation, human oversight workflows |
| **Urgency** | 🟡 **LGPD already enforceable; AI Bill approaching** |

| **Key Obligations** | Fairness, Ethics, Accountability, Transparency (FEAT). Explainability, reproducibility, auditability of AI models |

### 8. AUSTRALIA — APRA CPS 230 + AI Ethics Principles

2. **SBS actively supervising** — financial institutions face immediate compliance pressure
3. **KPMG channel available** — existing advisory relationships create a warm introduction path
4. **CendiaGateway fit** — reverse-proxy deployment requires zero changes to existing AI systems
5. **Federation model** — FEPCMAC's 11 cajas create built-in expansion after initial POC

7. **Regional expansion** — Peru success creates playbook for Colombia, Chile, Mexico
