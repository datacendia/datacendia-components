## Strategic Context

Peru is the fastest path to a first paying customer because:

1. **Regulation is already active** — DS N° 115-2025-PCM and Ley 31814 create non-discretionary compliance spend today
2. **SBS is actively supervising** — financial institutions can't defer AI governance
3. **KPMG connection available** — warm introduction through existing advisory relationships
4. **CendiaGateway requires zero integration** — deploys as reverse proxy in front of existing AI systems
5. **FEPCMAC creates a federation multiplier** — 1 POC → 11 cajas → annual enterprise contract
6. **Spanish-language materials are ready** — POC proposal, compliance statement, DPIA template

## Value-at-Risk Messaging

**Core message:** "If SBS requests evidence of AI governance today, what do you present?"

### For Credit Officers / Business

> "Your credit scoring AI makes thousands of decisions daily. Under Ley 31814, each one must have an audit trail showing transparency, explainability, and non-discrimination. Today, if SBS asks for that evidence, you have nothing to show. CendiaGateway generates it automatically — no changes to your existing AI systems."

### For Compliance / Risk Teams

> "DS N° 115-2025-PCM adopts ISO/IEC 42001 as mandatory. Your current risk management framework covers operational risk, credit risk, market risk — but has no technical controls for AI risk. CendiaGateway adds the AI governance layer to your existing risk infrastructure. One deployment, all AI systems covered."

### For CTO / Technology

> "CendiaGateway deploys as a reverse proxy — no code changes, no API modifications, no model retraining. It sits in front of your AI systems and captures everything. Sovereign deployment means no data leaves your infrastructure. Setup takes days, not months."

### For Board / C-Suite

> "Every Peruvian financial institution deploying AI faces a compliance gap between what SBS expects and what you can demonstrate today. The first SBS enforcement action will trigger urgent remediation across the sector. Being proactive converts a compliance cost into a competitive advantage — the first bank with ISO 42001 certification can use it as a trust signal with customers and regulators."
