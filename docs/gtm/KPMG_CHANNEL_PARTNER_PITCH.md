## The Opportunity for KPMG

Peru's DS N° 115-2025-PCM makes ISO/IEC 42001:2023 the **mandatory standard** for AI governance. Ley 31814 classifies credit scoring, judicial AI, and public services as **high-risk**. Every KPMG client deploying AI in Peru now needs:

1. A certified AI management system (AIMS)
2. Technical evidence of compliance — not just policies on paper
3. Auditable records for SBS inspections

**KPMG advises. Datacendia generates the evidence.**

This is not a competitive product — it's infrastructure that makes KPMG's advisory engagements more valuable and easier to deliver.

---

## The Product: CendiaGateway™

CendiaGateway is a **reverse-proxy** that sits between an organization's AI systems and their users. It:

| Capability | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Intercepts** | Captures every AI interaction without modifying existing systems |
| **Audits** | Detects PII (10 types), applies configurable governance policies |
| **Signs** | Cryptographically signs each record with SHA-256 — immutable, third-party verifiable |
| **Generates** | Produces a DS N° 115-2025-PCM evidence package ready for SBS or auditor presentation |

**Key differentiator:** Zero integration required. Deploys in front of any AI system (ChatGPT, Claude, internal models). Data never leaves the client's infrastructure.
