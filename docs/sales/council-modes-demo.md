# Council Modes Demo Script
## Same Question, Three Different Modes

**Demo Question:** "Should we hire 5 more engineers for Q1?"

---

## ⚔️ MODE 1: WAR ROOM
*"Conflict before Consensus"*

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  THE COUNCIL - WAR ROOM MODE                                    │
│  Query: Should we hire 5 more engineers for Q1?                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

👔 CHIEF STRATEGY AGENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Opening assessment: This is a $750K annual commitment assuming 
$150K fully-loaded cost per engineer. Initiating cross-functional 
debate.

💰 CFO AGENT (Finnley McTavish)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Framework: GAAP, Cash Flow Analysis

AGAINST aggressive hiring:
• Current burn rate: $2.1M/month
• Runway: 14 months
• Adding 5 FTEs reduces runway to 11.2 months
• Q1 revenue projection confidence: 65%
• Risk Score: 7/10

"We're betting runway on revenue we haven't closed. I need to see 
signed contracts, not pipeline."

⚙️ COO AGENT (Liam Chen)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Framework: Capacity Planning

FOR hiring:
• Current engineering utilization: 94%
• Sprint velocity declining 8% MoM for 3 months
• 3 critical features blocked on capacity
• Customer churn attributed to missing features: $180K ARR

"We're already underwater. Every month we delay costs us customers."

⚔️ CROSS-EXAMINATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CFO challenges COO: "Liam, you're citing $180K churn, but hiring 
5 engineers costs $750K. That's a 4:1 ratio. How do you justify 
that math?"

COO responds: "The $180K is trailing. Pipeline at risk due to 
feature gaps is $2.1M. We're not hiring to stop bleeding—we're 
hiring to close deals."

CFO counters: "Show me the pipeline. Which specific deals? Which 
features? I won't approve headcount against hypothetical revenue."

CTO interjects: "I can provide the list. 4 deals totaling $1.8M 
are contingent on the analytics dashboard shipping Q1. Current 
team can't deliver it before Q3."

Risk Agent (Aurora): "If we hire and revenue doesn't materialize, 
we face layoffs in Q3. Reputational damage, severance costs, 
remaining team morale impact. I estimate 30% probability of this 
scenario. Expected cost: $450K."

🎯 SYNTHESIS (Chief Strategy Agent)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DECISION: CONDITIONAL APPROVAL

Hire 3 engineers immediately, with 2 additional approved upon:
• Closing 2 of the 4 identified pipeline deals ($900K minimum)
• Deadline: February 15

Rationale:
• Addresses COO's capacity crisis (reduces utilization to 82%)
• Manages CFO's runway concern (13.1 months vs 11.2)
• Risk-adjusted: If deals close, we accelerate. If not, we've 
  limited exposure.

Action Items:
1. CTO: Submit job reqs for 3 engineers by EOD Friday
2. CFO: Model cash flow scenarios for both outcomes
3. CRO: Prioritize the 4 identified deals, weekly updates to Council
4. HR: Begin sourcing immediately, target start date Feb 1

Confidence: 75%
Risk Score: 5/10 (reduced from 7/10)
```

The alternative of waiting has a clear negative ROI due to

Expected return: $1,632,000
Risk-adjusted confidence: 72%

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Aspect | ⚔️ War Room | 💰 Investment | 🚨 Crisis |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| **Frame** | Debate the decision | Analyze the ROI | Execute the solution |
| **Tone** | Confrontational | Analytical | Urgent |
| **Output** | Conditional approval with debate record | Financial model with scenarios | Action table with owners/deadlines |
| **Time Focus** | Medium-term tradeoffs | ROI over 12 months | Next 1-24 hours |
| **Key Question** | "What are the risks?" | "What's the return?" | "Who does what, when?" |
| **Best For** | Contested decisions | Budget requests | Emergencies |
