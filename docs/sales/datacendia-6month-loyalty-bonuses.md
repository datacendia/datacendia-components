# 🎖️ Datacendia: 6-Month Loyalty Bonus Program

**The Surprise That Locks In Renewals**

At the 6-month mark, every client receives TWO industry-specific bonus service packages they didn't know were coming. These are more substantial than the welcome bonus - designed to demonstrate deep value and make renewal a no-brainer.

---

## The Psychology

**Why 6 Months:**
- Past the "new purchase" phase
- Starting to think about renewal
- Have enough usage data to personalize
- Competitors are sniffing around
- Internal champions need ammunition

**Why 2 Packages:**
- One for THEIR role (personal value)
- One for ANOTHER team (spreads internally)
- Creates two "wow" moments
- Generates two internal conversations

---

## Program Structure

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Timing** | Month 6, Day 1-7 |
| **Delivery** | Personal call from CSM, followed by email |
| **Format** | Two distinct deliverables |
| **Value Perception** | $30-75K each (total $60-150K) |
| **Actual Cost** | 4-8 hours Council time per package |

---

## Delivery Script (CSM Call)

```
"Hey [NAME], you've been with us 6 months now. Before I get into our 
regular check-in, I wanted to share something.

We've been watching how your team uses the Council, and we built 
two things specifically for you. These aren't part of your contract - 
they're a thank you for being a great customer.

The first one is for you personally: [PACKAGE 1 NAME]. 

The second one is for your [OTHER TEAM] - I thought [COLLEAGUE NAME] 
might find it useful: [PACKAGE 2 NAME].

I'll send both over after this call. No strings attached."
```
