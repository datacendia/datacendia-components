# CendiaGateway™ — AI Governance for Regulated Institutions

## What is CendiaGateway?

CendiaGateway is a governance layer that deploys between your institution's AI systems and your end users. It intercepts, audits, and cryptographically signs every AI interaction — generating verifiable technical evidence of regulatory compliance. It is not a chatbot. It is not a productivity tool. It is regulatory proof infrastructure.

## How does it deploy?

CendiaGateway installs inside your institution's infrastructure — on your servers, under your control, with your cryptographic keys. No data leaves your perimeter. Deployment takes less than one week and requires no modification to your existing AI systems. It operates as a reverse proxy: your current AI applications continue working exactly as before, but now every interaction is recorded, signed, and auditable.

## What does it generate?

A **Regulatory Evidence Package** containing:

- **Immutable record** of every AI interaction (who asked what, what the model responded, when)
- **SHA-256 cryptographic signature** per record — third-party verifiable, tamper-resistant
- **Automatic PII detection** — sensitive personal information identified and protected before reaching the model
- **AI Manifest™** — compliance artifact documenting policies applied, PII detected, model used, and outcome
- **Digital chain of custody** — full traceability from request to response

## Which regulations does it address?

| Regulation | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **EU AI Act (2024/1689)** | Article 12 technical documentation and record-keeping for high-risk AI systems |
| **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** | AI Management System — CendiaGateway generates the required technical evidence |
| **DS N° 115-2025-PCM** | Mandatory AI governance standard in Peru (adoption of ISO/IEC 42001:2023) |
| **NIST AI RMF 1.0** | US AI risk management framework — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage functions |
| **GDPR** | Data protection — automatic PII detection and sovereign deployment (zero data egress) |
| **SOC 2** | Security, availability, processing integrity — cryptographic audit trails |
