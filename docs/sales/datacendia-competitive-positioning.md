## COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE TRIGGERS

### News to Watch For

| Event | Implication | Your Move |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| **Competitor raises prices** | Customers looking for alternatives | Outbound campaign: "Tired of paying Palantir prices?" |
| **Competitor has outage/breach** | Trust damaged | Reference your sovereign deployment |
| **Competitor announces feature** | Validate the category | "Great to see [competitor] validating the need. Here's how we're different..." |
| **Customer loses deal to competitor** | Learn and adapt | Post-mortem: what did they offer that we didn't? |

### Win/Loss Analysis Template

When you win:
- What resonated most?
- Which persona drove the decision?
- What competitors were considered?
- What was the tipping point?

When you lose:
- What was the primary objection?
- Was it price, features, relationship, or timing?
- What would have changed the outcome?
- Can we stay in touch for future opportunity?
