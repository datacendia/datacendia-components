# Datacendia Demo Video Script
## 3-Minute Product Overview

**Target:** Investors, Enterprise Buyers, Design Partners  
**Tone:** Professional, confident, not salesy  
**Length:** 2:30 - 3:00

---

## INTRO (0:00 - 0:20)

**[Screen: Datacendia logo on dark background]**

> "Every day, executives make decisions worth millions—sometimes billions. But they're doing it with spreadsheets, gut instinct, and meetings that go nowhere."

**[Screen: Quick cuts of typical executive frustration - emails, meetings, dashboards]**

> "What if you had a council of AI advisors—each an expert in their domain—deliberating on your behalf, 24/7?"

**[Screen: Datacendia Council interface fades in]**

> "This is Datacendia."

---

## THE COUNCIL (0:20 - 1:00)

**[Screen: Council page with agent avatars]**

> "Meet The Council. Fourteen specialized AI agents—CFO, CISO, Legal, Risk, Ethics—each with distinct expertise and perspective."

**[Action: Type a question: "Should we acquire TechStartup Inc for $50M?"]**

> "Let's ask them a real question."

**[Screen: Agents begin responding in real-time, streaming responses]**

> "Watch as each agent analyzes from their domain. The CFO examines financials. The CISO flags security risks. Legal spots regulatory concerns."

**[Screen: Cross-examination phase begins]**

> "They don't just give opinions—they challenge each other. The Risk Agent questions the CFO's assumptions. The Devil's Advocate finds holes in the plan."

**[Screen: Synthesis appears]**

> "Finally, a synthesized recommendation with confidence scores and dissenting views documented."

---

## DECISION DNA (1:00 - 1:30)

**[Screen: Decision DNA / Ledger view]**

> "Every decision is recorded in an immutable ledger. Who participated. What evidence was considered. What alternatives were rejected."

**[Screen: Zoom into a decision packet]**

> "Cryptographically signed. forensic-grade, independently verifiable. Regulator-ready."

**[Screen: Show audit trail]**

> "Six months from now, when someone asks 'why did we make that call?'—you'll have the answer."

## CHRONOS (1:30 - 1:50)

**[Screen: Chronos time-travel interface]**

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Screencasts Needed
1. Council deliberation (full flow, 60 seconds)
2. Decision DNA / audit trail (15 seconds)
3. Chronos timeline (15 seconds)
4. Vertical/enterprise features montage (10 seconds)

### Voiceover
- Professional male or female voice
- Measured pace, confident tone
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing")

### Music
- Subtle, corporate, builds slightly during Council section
- Fades under for speaking sections

### Graphics
- Use actual product UI (not mockups)
- Agent avatars should be visible and recognizable
- Dark theme preferred for premium feel

### B-Roll (Optional)
- Executive in meeting (looking frustrated)
- Boardroom table
- Data visualizations
