# DATACENDIA: 3-MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT

## Recording Instructions
- **Length:** 3:00 - 3:30 max
- **Tone:** Confident, not salesy. You're showing, not selling.
- **Setup:** Screen recording with voiceover (or face in corner)
- **Music:** Subtle ambient/tech soundtrack, low volume

---

## SCRIPT

### [0:00 - 0:20] HOOK

**[SCREEN: Black screen with Datacendia logo fade-in]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Every enterprise runs on decisions. But most decisions are made with incomplete data, siloed opinions, and no audit trail.
>
> What if your organization had a mind of its own?
>
> This is Datacendia."

---

### [0:20 - 0:45] THE PROBLEM

**[SCREEN: Quick cuts of typical enterprise chaos - spreadsheets, email threads, meeting overload]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Today, strategic decisions bounce between inboxes. Finance says yes, Legal says wait, Security wasn't even asked. By the time you decide, the market moved.
>
> Datacendia changes that. It's not a dashboard. It's not a chatbot. It's an operating system for enterprise intelligence."

---

### [0:45 - 1:30] THE COUNCIL IN ACTION

**[SCREEN: Live demo of The Council - show the EU expansion deliberation]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Let me show you. I'll ask a simple question: 'Should we expand into the European market in Q4?'
>
> Watch what happens."

**[SCREEN: Show agents responding in real-time]**

> "Our CFO agent analyzes capital requirements - $4.2 million, recommends currency hedging.
>
> But then our CISO agent raises a *blocking concern*. GDPR compliance is incomplete. We can't proceed.
>
> These agents don't just answer. They *deliberate*. They challenge each other."

**[SCREEN: Show CendiaChief synthesis - "CONDITIONAL NO"]**

> "The Chief synthesizes: Conditional No. Financials approved, Security blocked. Recommendation: prioritize compliance, re-evaluate in 14 days.
>
> That's not a guess. That's a reasoned decision with a clear audit trail. In 30 seconds."

---

### [1:30 - 2:15] THE FIVE PILLARS

**[SCREEN: Quick tour through each pillar - 8-10 seconds each]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "The Council is just one part. Datacendia has five integrated spaces:"

**[SCREEN: The Graph]**
> "**The Graph** - trace any metric back to its source. You can't trust what you can't trace."

**[SCREEN: The Pulse]**
> "**The Pulse** - real-time organizational health. Anomalies detected before they become crises."

**[SCREEN: The Lens]**
> "**The Lens** - scenario modeling. What happens to revenue if supply chain fails? See the future."

**[SCREEN: The Bridge]**
> "**The Bridge** - turn decisions into actions. Automated workflows with human approval gates."

**[SCREEN: Return to Council]**
> "And **The Council** - where your AI C-suite debates before they advise."

---

### [2:15 - 2:45] THE DIFFERENTIATOR

**[SCREEN: Architecture diagram or "Sovereign" deployment visual]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "But here's what matters most: **Sovereignty**.
>

>

### [2:45 - 3:00] CLOSE

**[SCREEN: Datacendia logo + tagline]**
