## The 60-Second Pitch

Every year, **$3.1 trillion in enterprise decisions** are made with no audit trail, no structured deliberation, and no way to prove to regulators, boards, or courts that the decision was sound.

Datacendia is **decision verification infrastructure** — not an AI that makes decisions, but a system that proves *humans* made decisions correctly. We give regulated institutions (banks, hospitals, defense contractors) a **cryptographically-signed, replay-capable record** of every decision, who was consulted, what was considered, and why.

Think of it as **"black box recorder for enterprise decisions"** — like aircraft flight recorders, but for the decisions that move billions.
