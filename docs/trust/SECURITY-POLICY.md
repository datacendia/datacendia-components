# Datacendia Security Disclosure Policy

**Effective Date:** February 6, 2026  
**Contact:** security@datacendia.com  

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all Datacendia software, services, and infrastructure.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in any Datacendia product or service, please report it to us responsibly.

**Email:** security@datacendia.com

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Affected component or version (if known)
- Any potential impact assessment

## What We Commit To

- **Acknowledgement** within 3 business days of receipt
- **Initial assessment** within 10 business days
- **Status updates** at least every 14 days until resolution
- **Credit** in our security acknowledgements (unless you prefer anonymity)

## What We Ask

- Do not access, modify, or delete data belonging to other users
- Do not degrade or disrupt service availability
- Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have had reasonable time to address it
- Act in good faith

## Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action against security researchers who:

- Act in good faith and in accordance with this policy
- Avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption
- Report findings promptly and confidentially

## Out of Scope

- Social engineering attacks against Datacendia employees
- Physical security attacks
- Denial of service attacks
- Spam or phishing

## Recognition

We maintain a security acknowledgements page for researchers who responsibly disclose valid vulnerabilities. We do not currently operate a paid bug bounty program.
