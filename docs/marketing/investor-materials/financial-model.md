## Key Assumptions

1. **No revenue from white-label/channel in Year 1** — conservative
2. **Pilot → Foundation conversion: 60%** — based on enterprise software benchmarks
3. **Foundation → Enterprise expansion: 40%** in Year 2 of contract
4. **Sales cycle:** 90 days (pilot), 6 months (foundation), 9 months (enterprise)
5. **Sales team productivity:** $1.5M quota/rep (Year 2+), 70% attainment
6. **Marketing:** 30% of pipeline from inbound/content, 50% from outbound, 20% from channel
