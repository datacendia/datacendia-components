## FAQ

**Q: Is this SaaS?**
No. Datacendia is annual-licensed software deployed on your infrastructure. Your data, your keys, your proof. We never see your data.

**Q: Can we try before we buy?**
Yes — the $50K DCII Pilot is a 90-day proof of concept. It converts to a Foundation or Enterprise license at contract value.

**Q: What infrastructure do we need?**
Core deployment: 8GB RAM (PostgreSQL, Redis, Neo4j, Ollama). Full enterprise: 32-96GB depending on profile. Docker Compose or Kubernetes. See deployment guide.

**Q: Do you support air-gapped environments?**
Yes — air-gapped deployment is a core capability. Data diode, QR air-gap bridge, TPM attestation, bootable USB. No internet required.

**Q: What AI models do you use?**
You choose. 8 configurable model slots. Default: Ollama with open-weight models (Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek). Optional: NVIDIA Triton, NIM. Your models, on your hardware.

**Q: How long does deployment take?**
Pilot: 2-4 weeks to first decision. Foundation: 4-8 weeks. Enterprise: 8-16 weeks for full deployment.
