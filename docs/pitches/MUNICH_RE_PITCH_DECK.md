## Slide 7 — Munich Re Scenario

### Example: Emerging Risk — Cyber Accumulation Decision

**Scenario:**

Large corporate cyber program submission. Existing cyber portfolio approaching accumulation limits.
- Technical price attractive
- Accumulation model shows concentration risk
- Risk appetite requires senior approval

**The governance question:**
- How was accumulation risk assessed?
- Why was this risk accepted despite concentration?
- What conditions or limits were applied?
- Is there defensible evidence?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Submission analysis captured with accumulation data
2. Risk appetite threshold documented
3. Mitigating factors recorded (reinsurance, conditions, limits)
4. Senior underwriter rationale captured
5. Approval with attribution and timestamp
6. Immutable artifact created

---

**"When cyber becomes the next catastrophe, Munich Re can prove its accumulation governance."**

# Objection Handling

## "We have robust underwriting governance already."

> "You do. Datacendia doesn't replace it — it creates systematic evidence that governance was applied to each decision. When BaFin asks about a specific underwriting decision from 2026, you have the proof."

## "Our scale makes this challenging."

> "Designed for scale. Governance depth is configurable by decision materiality. Routine renewals get lightweight capture; complex risks get structured deliberation. The system scales with your volume."

## "How does this work across our regions?"

> "Consistent evidence format across Munich, London, Princeton, Singapore. Regional teams work in familiar workflows; central governance gets unified reporting. One platform, global visibility."
