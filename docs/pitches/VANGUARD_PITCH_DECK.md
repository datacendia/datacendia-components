### Example: Factor Tilt Decision in Retirement Fund

**Scenario:**

Quantitative model suggests factor tilt adjustment in target-date fund. Decision affects millions of retirement savers.

Questions that will be asked:
- Was this in the best interest of participants?
- Who made the decision and on what basis?
- Were risks to participants surfaced?
- Was there any dissent?

**What Datacendia does:**

1. Structures deliberation: Investment rationale, risk assessment, fiduciary review
2. Records each perspective with attribution
3. Captures dissent with full rationale
4. Produces signed artifact before implementation

# Objection Handling

## "Our fiduciary processes are already robust."

> "They are. Datacendia doesn't replace them — it creates evidence that they were followed for each specific decision. The question isn't whether you have fiduciary processes. It's whether you can prove a specific decision in 2026 went through them when asked in 2029."

## "How is this different from our committee documentation?"

> "Committee minutes record that a meeting happened. Datacendia structures the deliberation itself — capturing reasoning, dissent, and attestation in real-time, with cryptographic integrity. It's the difference between 'we discussed this' and 'here's exactly how we decided.'"

## "Does this add overhead?"

> "Governance levels are configurable. Routine decisions get lightweight capture. Material decisions affecting participants get structured deliberation. You control where the rigor applies."
