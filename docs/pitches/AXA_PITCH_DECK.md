## Slide 3 — The EU AI Act Challenge

### What High-Risk Classification Means

| Requirement | What It Means | Current State |
|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| Human oversight | Documented human review of AI outputs | Ad hoc |
| Record-keeping | Complete decision trail | Fragmented |
| Transparency | Explain how AI was used | Technical only |
| Risk management | Document AI risk mitigation | Policy-level |

**The gap:**

- You have AI policies
- You need AI governance evidence for each decision

**"The EU AI Act doesn't ask if you have AI governance. It asks you to prove it."**

## Slide 7 — AXA Scenario

### Example: AI-Assisted Underwriting Decision with Override

**Scenario:**

AI underwriting model recommends decline for commercial property risk. Underwriter believes model is missing context:
- Long-standing broker relationship
- Recent risk improvements not in data
- Business case for exception

**The governance question:**
- Why was AI recommendation overridden?
- Was human oversight exercised appropriately?
- Is there evidence of responsible AI use?

**With Datacendia:**

1. AI recommendation captured with model version
2. Underwriter assessment documented
3. Override rationale recorded: specific reasons, evidence cited
4. Risk acknowledgment captured
5. Approval with attribution
6. Immutable evidence created

# Objection Handling

## "We already have AI governance policies."

> "Policies are necessary but not sufficient. The EU AI Act requires evidence that governance was applied to each decision. Datacendia creates that evidence — documenting human oversight, override rationale, and decision trails for each AI-assisted decision."

## "Our AI is explainable."

> "Model explainability is technical. Governance evidence is procedural. Regulators want to see that humans reviewed AI outputs, exercised judgment, and made accountable decisions. That's what Datacendia captures."

## "Will this slow down our AI processes?"

> "The governance capture runs parallel to decision processes. For most decisions, it's lightweight. For material decisions or overrides, it adds 2-3 minutes of documentation. The alternative — having no evidence when regulators ask — is far more costly."
