## Slide 7 — State Street Scenario

### Example: Proxy Voting Decision with ESG Implications

**Scenario:**

Major corporate action requires proxy vote for institutional clients. Issues:
- ESG implications vary by client mandate
- Significant reputational risk either way
- Multiple stakeholders must be consulted

**The governance question:**
- How was the voting decision made?
- Were client mandates considered?
- Who had input and what was their view?
- Is there a defensible record?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Decision initiated with client mandate context
2. ESG analysis captured
3. Stakeholder input recorded (Investment, Client Service, Legal)
4. Final decision with rationale documented
5. Immutable artifact linked to proxy record

---

**"When a client asks why you voted that way, you have structured evidence — not just a policy."**
