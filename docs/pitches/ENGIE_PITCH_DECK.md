## Slide 7 — ENGIE Scenario: EU Taxonomy Classification

### Example: Investment Classification Decision

**Scenario:**

Major infrastructure investment. Must classify under EU Taxonomy for reporting and potential green financing.

**What auditors and regulators will examine:**
- How was the activity classified?
- Were technical screening criteria properly applied?
- Who made the classification decision?
- Is there evidence of proper assessment?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Investment details documented
2. Taxonomy criteria assessment captured
3. Technical screening analysis recorded
4. DNSH (Do No Significant Harm) assessment documented
5. Classification decision with rationale
6. Reviewer sign-off captured
7. Immutable evidence created
