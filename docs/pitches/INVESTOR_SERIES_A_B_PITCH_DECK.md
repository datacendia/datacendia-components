# Investor Q&A Prep

## "Why won't incumbents build this?"

> "GRC platforms are built around policies and controls, not decisions. Rebuilding for decision-level governance would require architectural changes and new expertise. By the time they ship, we'll have the compliance framework depth and customer relationships."

## "How defensible is this?"

> "Three moats: (1) Compliance framework mapping — 20+ frameworks, deep expertise. (2) Cryptographic evidence chain — legally tested architecture. (3) Enterprise deployment — on-prem and sovereign options most startups can't offer."

## "What's the sales cycle?"

> "6-9 months for initial land, with strong expansion after. Department-level entry reduces friction. CFO budget for compliance, not IT."

## "Why not build in-house?"

> "Enterprises try and fail. They lack compliance framework expertise, cryptographic evidence architecture, and regulatory relationships. Build vs. buy analysis strongly favors buy."
