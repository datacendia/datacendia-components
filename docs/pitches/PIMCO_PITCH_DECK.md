## Slide 2 — PIMCO's Challenge

### Credit Decisions Under Pressure

- Fixed income involves judgment calls that models can't fully capture
- EM sovereign debt, distressed credit, duration bets — all require human expertise
- But expertise exercised under time pressure is hard to document

**The scrutiny vectors:**

1. **Regulators** — How did you assess credit risk?
2. **Investors** — Why did you increase EM exposure before the event?
3. **Litigators** — Was the decision prudent given available information?

## Slide 3 — The Documentation Gap

### What Gets Lost in Fast-Moving Markets

| When markets move | What happens | What's documented |
|-------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Credit event unfolds | PM makes rapid assessment | Trade ticket |
| Opportunity window opens | Risk discussion happens verbally | Email fragments |
| Position taken | Decision made in minutes | After-the-fact rationale |

---

**"In credit, the best decisions are made fast. The worst documentation is too."**

## Slide 4 — What Regulators Now Expect

### The New Standard

**SEC / FINRA:**
- Investment advisers must document decision rationale
- "Best execution" extends to decision process
- AI-assisted decisions require human oversight proof

**EU MiFID II:**
- Suitability documentation requirements
- Record-keeping for investment decisions
- Audit trail expectations increasing

---

**"Regulators don't accept 'we had a view' anymore. They want to see the process."**

# Objection Handling

## "We don't have time for documentation in fast markets."

> "That's exactly the problem we solve. Traditional documentation happens after the fact and gets reconstructed. Datacendia captures in real-time — 2-3 minutes — and doesn't block execution. The alternative is having no contemporaneous evidence when you need it most."

## "Our credit analysts already document their views."

> "They do — but often after the decision, in formats that aren't legally defensible. Datacendia captures reasoning before execution, with timestamps and integrity verification. That's what distinguishes 'we had a view' from 'here's proof we assessed the risks.'"

## "How does this work with our trading systems?"

> "API integration with your OMS and compliance workflows. Decision records link to trade IDs. The capture process runs parallel to execution, not serial."
