## Slide 7 — Kaiser Scenario

### Example: Care Decision at Coverage Boundary

**Scenario:**

Member requests advanced imaging. Primary care physician assessment suggests watchful waiting may be appropriate. Question: imaging now or wait?

In an integrated model, this decision will be scrutinized:
- Was clinical judgment or cost the driver?
- Did the physician recommend against the member's preference?
- Is there evidence of patient-centered reasoning?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Clinical presentation documented
2. Physician assessment captured: clinical reasoning for recommendation
3. Patient discussion documented: shared decision-making
4. Decision recorded with rationale: why this serves patient's clinical interest
5. Follow-up plan captured
6. Immutable evidence created

---

**"When asked 'was this about cost?', you have evidence it was about care."**

# Objection Handling

## "Our integrated model already ensures patient-centered care."

> "It does. Datacendia creates evidence of that care — so when regulators, members, or anyone else asks, you can prove decisions served the patient. Trust is built on transparency."

## "Our clinicians are already documenting in Epic."

> "Epic captures clinical care. Datacendia captures decision governance — the reasoning, the shared decision-making, the evidence-based basis. That's what transparency requires and regulators increasingly expect."

## "This seems like administrative burden."

> "The capture is lightweight — it documents what clinicians are already thinking and discussing. The alternative — having no evidence when your integrated model is questioned — is far more burdensome."
