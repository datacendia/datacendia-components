## Slide 4 — The Regulatory Trajectory

### Where This Is Heading

**SEC (2024-2026):**
- Proposed rules on AI in investment advice
- Focus on conflicts, explainability, human oversight

**EU AI Act (2025-2027):**
- Financial AI classified as high-risk
- Requires human oversight documentation
- Mandates audit trails

**DOL / ERISA:**
- Fiduciary duty extends to AI-assisted decisions
- Prudence must be provable

## Slide 7 — BlackRock Scenario

### Example: ESG Mandate Compliance Decision

**Scenario:**

Aladdin flags an opportunity in an EM energy company. Portfolio is subject to ESG mandate.

Decision required:
- Does this align with stated ESG criteria?
- Who decided, and on what basis?
- Was there dissent from ESG team?

**What Datacendia does:**

1. Structures deliberation across PM, Risk, ESG teams
2. Records each perspective with attribution
3. Captures dissent (if any) with rationale
4. Produces signed artifact before execution

# Objection Handling

## "We already have extensive compliance processes."

> "Exactly. Datacendia doesn't replace those — it creates evidence that they were followed for each specific decision. The question regulators ask isn't 'do you have processes?' It's 'can you prove this decision went through them?'"

## "How does this work with Aladdin?"

> "Datacendia sits alongside Aladdin via API hooks. When Aladdin generates a recommendation, Datacendia structures the human deliberation that follows. We don't duplicate or compete — we create the governance layer that Aladdin doesn't provide."

## "This seems like it could slow things down."

> "Governance levels are configurable. Routine decisions get lightweight capture. Material decisions — the ones that matter under scrutiny — get structured deliberation. You control the threshold."

## "What about our existing audit trails?"

> "Audit trails record what happened. Datacendia structures what should happen before execution — deliberation, dissent, accountability. That's the difference between a flight recorder and a pre-flight checklist."
