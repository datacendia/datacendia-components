## Slide 7 — UnitedHealth Scenario

### Example: Prior Authorization with AI Triage

**Scenario:**

Prior authorization request for specialty drug. AI system triages as likely denial based on clinical criteria. Medical director review required.

**The legal question (asked in discovery):**
- Did a human actually review this case?
- What clinical information was considered?
- Was the AI recommendation blindly followed?
- Is there evidence of individualized determination?

**With Datacendia:**

1. AI triage recommendation captured with model version
2. Case materials reviewed documented
3. Medical director assessment recorded: clinical reasoning
4. Decision rationale captured: specific to patient situation
5. Individualized determination evidenced
6. Immutable artifact created before determination issued

# Objection Handling

## "We already document our decisions."

> "You document outcomes. But can you prove, for a specific determination in January 2026, that a human clinician reviewed the case materials, exercised clinical judgment, and made an individualized determination? That's what plaintiff's attorneys and CMS auditors ask for."

## "This could slow down claims processing."

> "Governance depth is configurable. Standard approvals get lightweight capture. Denials and appeals — the decisions that face scrutiny — get full documentation. The system runs parallel to your existing workflow."

## "How does this work with our claims systems?"

> "API integration with your existing platforms. Datacendia captures the human oversight layer; your systems handle processing. No workflow disruption."

## "What about AI-only decisions?"

> "Even automated approvals should have governance evidence — showing the criteria were appropriate and human oversight exists at the policy level. For any adverse determination, human review documentation is essential."
