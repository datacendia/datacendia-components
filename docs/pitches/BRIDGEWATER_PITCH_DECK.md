## Slide 7 — Bridgewater Scenario

### Example: Model Override Decision

**Scenario:**

Systematic model signals significant position change. Portfolio managers assess:
- Model assumptions may not reflect current regime
- Principles suggest caution in novel situations
- Decision: Partial implementation with modified sizing

**The governance question:**
- Why was the model not followed fully?
- What principles justified the override?
- Who decided, and based on what reasoning?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Model signal captured with timestamp
2. PM reasoning documented: "Current regime differs from training data"
3. Principle citation: "Principle 4.2 — Adapt to what you don't know"
4. Risk assessment recorded
5. Decision rationale immutably stored

---

**"When asked why you deviated from the model, you have the principled answer — with proof."**

# Objection Handling

## "We already have radical transparency internally."

> "Internal transparency is your strength. Datacendia creates external defensibility — the kind that holds up under regulatory examination or litigation. The Dot Collector captures perspectives; Datacendia creates legally admissible evidence."

## "Our systematic process is well-documented."

> "The process is documented. But can you prove a specific decision on January 30, 2026 followed that process, applied the relevant principles, and included appropriate human judgment? That's what regulators and litigators will ask."

## "How does this work with our existing infrastructure?"

> "API integration. Datacendia can ingest signals from your models, reference your documented principles, and capture the human judgment applied. It's a layer, not a replacement."
