## Slide 7 — PGIM Scenario: Credit Committee Decision

### Example: High-Yield Position in Distressed Issuer

**Scenario:**

PGIM Fixed Income holds position in issuer showing credit deterioration. Credit committee meets to assess — hold, reduce, or exit.

Decision to maintain position with enhanced monitoring.

**What regulators and clients will ask:**
- Was the credit risk properly assessed?
- Was the decision consistent with mandate guidelines?
- Were dissenting views considered?
- Is there evidence of ongoing monitoring?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Credit analysis documented
2. Committee members' views captured
3. Deliberation recorded: bull case vs. bear case
4. Dissent (if any) documented with rationale
5. Decision with explicit reasoning
6. Monitoring requirements formalized
7. Immutable artifact created

---

**"When the SEC asks how you managed the credit risk, you have the evidence."**

# Objection Handling

## "Each affiliate has its own governance processes."

> "And they should. Datacendia doesn't standardize investment process — it creates consistent evidence of how each affiliate's governance was followed. Autonomy in decisions, consistency in documentation."

## "Investment committee minutes already capture this."

> "Minutes capture outcomes and summary. Datacendia captures the deliberation — the reasoning, the trade-offs considered, the dissenting views. When an SEC examiner asks 'how did you decide to hold that position?', you have the complete answer."

## "How does this work across asset classes?"

> "The platform is configurable for each decision context. Credit committee, investment committee, model governance — different structures, same evidence format. One platform across all affiliates."

## "Our PMs won't want another system."

> "Integration with existing workflow. Datacendia captures the deliberation that's already happening, structured for evidence. Not a parallel process — documentation of the existing process."
