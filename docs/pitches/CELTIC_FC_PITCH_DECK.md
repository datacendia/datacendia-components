## Slide 2 — Celtic's Decision Landscape

### High-Value Decisions Under Scrutiny

- Transfer decisions worth millions, examined by fans, media, and regulators
- Player contracts with complex structures and agent negotiations
- Commercial partnerships affecting brand and revenue
- Youth academy investments with long-term implications
- FFP/Club Licensing compliance requirements

**The accountability reality:**

1. **UEFA/SFA** — Club licensing, FFP documentation
2. **Board** — Fiduciary duty, investment justification
3. **Shareholders** — Return on transfer and commercial investments
4. **Supporters** — Transparency expectations (especially at Celtic)
5. **Agents/Clubs** — Dispute resolution, evidence in negotiations

## Slide 3 — The Transfer Decision Challenge

### Millions at Stake, Limited Documentation

| Decision | What Happens | What's Documented |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Player signing | Extensive scouting, negotiation | Final contract |
| Player sale | Valuation, negotiation | Sale agreement |
| Contract renewal | Performance review, market analysis | New contract |
| Loan decisions | Player development assessment | Loan terms |

**The gap:** Process and rationale often exist only in emails and memories.

---

**"When a £15M signing doesn't work out, can you show the due diligence was proper?"**

## Slide 4 — Regulatory and Governance Context

### What's Required and What's Coming

**Current:**
- UEFA Club Licensing (financial documentation)
- SFA registration requirements
- FFP/Sustainability regulations
- Companies Act fiduciary duties

**Emerging:**
- Enhanced FFP scrutiny post-pandemic
- Independent football regulator (UK)
- Increased agent regulation
- Greater transparency expectations

---

**"Football governance is professionalizing. Documentation standards are rising."**

## Slide 7 — Celtic Scenario: Player Acquisition

### Example: Significant Transfer Investment

**Scenario:**

Celtic identifies target player. Extensive scouting, data analysis, negotiation with selling club and agent. Board approval required for fee.

Investment: £8M fee + £3M agent fees + £40K/week wages.

**What stakeholders will ask:**
- What was the scouting process?
- How was the valuation determined?
- Were alternatives considered?
- Did the board properly approve?
- Was the agent fee appropriate?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Scouting reports and data analysis documented
2. Valuation methodology captured
3. Alternative targets considered and rationale for selection
4. Negotiation strategy and key decision points
5. Board deliberation and approval
6. Agent fee justification
7. Immutable record created before signing

# Objection Handling

## "Transfer windows move too fast for this."

> "The platform is designed for pace. Quick capture during scouting and negotiation, formal documentation before signing. The alternative — reconstructing rationale after a signing fails — is far more time-consuming."

## "This is confidential information."

> "Absolutely. On-premises deployment, no data leaves your infrastructure. Access controls ensure only appropriate personnel see sensitive information. Evidence exists for when you need it, not for public consumption."

## "We're not a regulated enterprise like a bank."

> "Football regulation is increasing — FFP, the independent regulator, agent rules. And beyond regulation, you have fiduciary duties to shareholders and accountability to supporters. Governance evidence protects the club."

## "Our scouts and recruitment team won't want paperwork."

> "Integrated capture, not paperwork. Scouting reports, data analysis, negotiation notes — they're already being created. Datacendia structures them into defensible evidence without duplicating work."
