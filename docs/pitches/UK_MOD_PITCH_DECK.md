## Slide 2 — The Defence Decision Challenge

### Decisions Under Exceptional Scrutiny

- Defence procurement decisions face NAO audit, PAC inquiry, and legal challenge
- Operational decisions may face judicial review, inquest, or international scrutiny
- AI assistance is increasing — but accountability must remain clear
- Every major decision may be examined years later, in adversarial contexts

**The accountability vectors:**

1. **NAO / PAC** — Value for money, process compliance
2. **Legal** — Judicial review, contract disputes, inquests
3. **International** — IHL compliance, coalition accountability
4. **Political** — Ministerial accountability, Parliamentary questions
5. **Public** — FOI, media scrutiny

---

**"In Defence, decisions made today will be examined under circumstances we cannot predict."**

## Slide 7 — MOD Scenario: Procurement

### Example: Major Equipment Programme Decision

**Scenario:**

Down-select decision in major equipment programme. Multiple bidders, significant investment, Parliamentary interest.

**What NAO/PAC will ask:**
- Were evaluation criteria applied consistently?
- How was the recommended option selected?
- Was dissent considered and addressed?
- Is there evidence of proper process?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Evaluation criteria documented with weights
2. Each bidder assessment recorded with evidence
3. Evaluation board deliberation captured
4. Dissenting views recorded with rationale
5. Final recommendation with complete reasoning
6. Immutable artifact created before decision announcement

---

**"When PAC asks how this decision was made, you have the complete record."**
