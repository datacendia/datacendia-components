# Objection Handling

## "We already have compliance processes."

> "Of course. Datacendia doesn't replace your processes — it creates evidence that your processes were followed. The question isn't whether you have controls. It's whether you can prove they were applied to a specific decision, two years later."

## "How is this different from our audit logs?"

> "Logs record what happened after the fact. Datacendia operates before execution — it structures deliberation, surfaces dissent, and creates evidence before the decision is made. That's the difference between a flight recorder and a pre-flight checklist."

## "Does this slow down decision-making?"

> "It structures what your teams already do. The deliberation process takes minutes, not hours. The alternative — being unable to defend a decision under scrutiny — is far more costly."

## "What if we disagree with the AI's assessment?"

> "The AI doesn't have an assessment. Datacendia records how humans evaluated the decision. If you override a risk flag, that override is recorded — with attribution, timestamp, and rationale. That's exactly what protects you."

## "Can we try this on a single workflow?"

> "Yes. We recommend starting with one high-scrutiny decision type — perhaps EM sovereign exposure or PEP-related transactions. That lets you evaluate the artifact quality before broader deployment."
