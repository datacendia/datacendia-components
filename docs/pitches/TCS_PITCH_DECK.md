## Slide 7 — TCS Scenario: AI Solution Delivery

### Example: AI-Powered Decision System for Financial Client

**Scenario:**

TCS delivers AI-powered credit decision system for bank client. Model makes recommendations, humans make final decisions.

**What regulators (and the client) will ask:**
- How is human oversight documented?
- Can you show the governance of model deployment decisions?
- Where's the evidence of proper testing decisions?
- How do you document production incidents and responses?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Model deployment decisions documented
2. Testing and validation decisions captured
3. Human oversight evidence recorded
4. Production decisions (overrides, incidents) documented
5. Immutable trail for client and regulator

# Objection Handling

## "We have our own governance frameworks and methodologies."

> "And they're excellent. Datacendia doesn't replace methodology — it creates decision-level evidence those methodologies were followed. When your client faces an audit, you have contemporaneous records, not reconstructed documentation."

## "Our clients each have different requirements."

> "The platform is configurable per client context. Same governance infrastructure, adapted to each client's regulatory framework and evidence requirements. One investment, multiple client applications."

## "How does this fit with our existing delivery tools?"

> "API integration with your delivery platforms, project management, and quality systems. Datacendia captures the deliberation layer; your existing tools handle the workflow."

## "Could this be a service offering?"

> "Absolutely. Decision governance consulting is a natural extension of your AI advisory practice. We can discuss partnership models that enable TCS to deliver governance as a service."
