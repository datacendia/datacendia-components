## Slide 7 — Elevance Scenario

### Example: Utilization Review with Appeal

**Scenario:**

Inpatient stay extension request. AI-assisted review suggests denial based on clinical criteria. Nurse reviewer and physician advisor involved.

Member appeals. Case goes to external review.

**What external reviewers and courts will ask:**
- What information was actually reviewed?
- Did qualified clinicians make the determination?
- Was the AI recommendation rubber-stamped?
- Is there evidence of individualized assessment?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Initial request and clinical materials documented
2. AI recommendation captured with basis
3. Nurse reviewer assessment recorded
4. Physician advisor clinical reasoning documented
5. Appeal review fully captured
6. Complete trail for external review

---

**"When the case goes external, you have the evidence trail."**
