## Slide 2 — The Alliance Decision Challenge

### Decisions That Must Satisfy 32 Nations

- NATO decisions involve multiple nations with different legal frameworks
- Coalition operations require demonstrable consensus
- AI assistance is increasing — accountability must remain clear across borders
- Decisions may face scrutiny from any member nation's parliament, courts, or public

**The accountability complexity:**

1. **NAC level** — Political consensus documentation
2. **Military Committee** — Military advice evidence
3. **SHAPE / ACO** — Operational decision trails
4. **National capitals** — Each nation's oversight requirements
5. **International law** — IHL compliance across coalition

## Slide 7 — NATO Scenario: Operational Planning

### Example: Coalition Mission Authorization

**Scenario:**

Mission planning for Alliance operation. Multiple contributing nations, varied national caveats, time pressure.

**What national oversight will ask:**
- How was the mission concept developed?
- Were national concerns heard?
- How were caveats accommodated?
- Is there evidence our nation's position was considered?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Mission concept documented with planning rationale
2. National inputs recorded (appropriately sanitized)
3. Deliberation captured: how concerns were addressed
4. Caveat accommodation documented
5. Consensus record with attribution by nation/role
6. Immutable artifact for each contributing nation

---

**"When a national parliament asks 'was our position heard?', you have the evidence."**

# Objection Handling

## "NATO already has established decision processes."

> "It does. Datacendia creates evidence those processes were followed — evidence that satisfies 32 different national accountability frameworks. When a national parliament asks how their position was considered, you have the record."

## "Security accreditation takes years."

> "We're prepared for that timeline. We can start with lower classification environments or with individual nations, building accreditation pathway in parallel with operational value demonstration."

## "Each nation has different requirements."

> "Exactly the problem we solve. One evidence format, configurable for each nation's oversight needs. The decision record serves as the single source of truth that each national system can draw from."
