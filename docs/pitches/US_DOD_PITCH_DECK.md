## Slide 7 — DoD Scenario: Source Selection

### Example: Competitive Acquisition with Technical Risk

**Scenario:**

Competitive award for major service contract. Three offerors, one with technical concerns, one with price concerns, one middle-ground.

Selection made for highest-rated, not lowest-priced.

**What GAO will ask in protest:**
- How was technical evaluation conducted?
- Was the cost/technical trade-off rational?
- Were all offerors treated equally?
- Is there evidence of the SSA's reasoning?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Evaluation criteria documented with SSEB approach
2. Each offeror assessment captured with evidence
3. SSEB deliberation recorded
4. Trade-off analysis documented: why technical risk matters more than price delta
5. SSA determination with complete reasoning
6. Immutable artifact created before award

# Objection Handling

## "We have CPARS, SAM, and existing systems."

> "Those systems record outcomes and contractor performance. Datacendia captures the decision process — how evaluations were conducted, why trade-offs were made, what the SSA's reasoning was. That's what sustains a protest."

## "Our contracting officers are already overwhelmed."

> "Datacendia integrates into the evaluation process, not on top of it. It structures deliberation that's already happening, capturing it in real-time rather than requiring after-the-fact documentation."

## "What about FedRAMP authorization?"

> "We're on the FedRAMP High authorization pathway. We can discuss timeline and current authorization status, and explore DoD sponsor options to accelerate if needed."
