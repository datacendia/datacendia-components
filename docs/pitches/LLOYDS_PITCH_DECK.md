## Slide 7 — Lloyd's Scenario

### Example: Large Risk Outside Normal Appetite

**Scenario:**

Attractive submission in specialty class. Risk characteristics outside standard appetite parameters:
- Geography outside core territory
- Limit above normal line size
- Novel risk features

Requires escalation and approval.

**The governance question:**
- Was authority framework followed?
- Who approved the exception?
- What was the underwriting rationale?
- Is there evidence for Performance Management review?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Submission analysis captured
2. Appetite deviation flagged automatically
3. Escalation documented
4. Senior underwriter rationale recorded
5. Approval with authority reference
6. Immutable artifact created before binding

---

**"When Performance Management asks about appetite adherence, you have decision-level evidence."**

# Objection Handling

## "Each syndicate has its own governance."

> "Exactly. Datacendia works within each syndicate's governance framework, evidencing adherence to their specific business plan and authorities. For managing agents, it provides consistent evidence format across syndicates."

## "How does this integrate with Lloyd's platforms?"

> "API integration with PPL and other Lloyd's systems. The governance capture happens alongside existing placement workflows. No disruption to current processes."

## "Performance Management already reviews our business."

> "They review outcomes and aggregates. Datacendia creates decision-level evidence — what you can show when they ask about a specific underwriting decision and how it aligned with your stated plan."
