## Slide 7 — Shell Scenario: Sustainability Investment Decision

### Example: Capital Allocation with Transition Implications

**Scenario:**

Investment committee decision on major project. Traditional energy asset, significant capital, long payback period. Questions about transition alignment.

**What stakeholders (and courts) will ask:**
- How was climate impact considered?
- Was transition risk assessed?
- Did dissenting views exist?
- Was this consistent with Net Zero commitments?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Investment proposal documented
2. Climate/transition analysis captured
3. Risk assessment recorded: stranded asset risk, regulatory risk
4. Investment committee deliberation captured
5. Dissent recorded (if any) with rationale
6. Final decision with explicit reasoning on transition alignment
7. Immutable artifact created

---

**"When your transition strategy is examined in court, you have decision-level evidence."**
