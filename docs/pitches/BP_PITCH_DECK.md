## Slide 7 — BP Scenario: Risk Acceptance Decision

### Example: Operational Risk Acceptance Under Time Pressure

**Scenario:**

Operational issue requires quick decision. Standard procedure would delay production. Risk assessment conducted, mitigation identified, decision needed.

**What investigators will ask (if anything goes wrong):**
- Was the risk properly assessed?
- Who made the decision and with what authority?
- Were alternatives considered?
- Was there any dissent?
- Was this a pattern or a one-time exception?

**With Datacendia:**

1. Issue documented with urgency context
2. Risk assessment captured
3. Mitigation measures recorded
4. Decision-maker's reasoning documented
5. Authority level confirmed
6. Dissent (if any) recorded
7. Immutable artifact created before action

---

**"If something goes wrong, you have evidence the decision was made responsibly."**

# Objection Handling

## "We've invested heavily in governance since [historical incidents]."

> "And it shows. Datacendia doesn't replace that investment — it creates evidence those governance processes were followed for each specific decision. When examined years later, you have contemporaneous records, not reconstructed rationale."

## "Our operations move fast. This seems like overhead."

> "The capture is designed for operational tempo. Risk acceptance decisions get documented in minutes, not hours. The alternative — having no evidence when something goes wrong — is far more costly."

## "How does this work with our existing MOC and permit systems?"

> "API integration. Datacendia captures the deliberation and decision-making; your existing systems handle workflow and compliance tracking. The human judgment layer is what we document."
