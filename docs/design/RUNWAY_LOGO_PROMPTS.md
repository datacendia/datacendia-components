## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS IN RUNWAY

1. **Be specific about composition** — Runway responds well to spatial descriptions ("centered", "viewed from above", "three-quarter angle")
2. **Specify what NOT to include** — Add "no text, no words, no letters" if you want icon-only output
3. **Iterate with variations** — Generate 4 variations per prompt, pick the best, then refine
4. **Upscale the winner** — Use Runway's upscaling to get production-quality resolution
5. **Consistency tip** — To maintain brand consistency across all icons, add "consistent with a geometric, clean-line enterprise design system" to each prompt
6. **Color control** — Runway sometimes interprets colors loosely. If cyan isn't right, try "teal blue #06B6D4" or "deep electric blue"
7. **Export at 2x** — Generate at the largest size available, then crop/resize for different use cases (favicon, sidebar icon, card header, etc.)
